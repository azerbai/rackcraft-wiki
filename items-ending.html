After the IPO

Spoilers. The Field Manual keeps this chapter for late in the game.

You went public. You knew there would be consequences; this is the page about them. While HEROBRINE-1 is loose he drains your Personal Funds, so the faster it ends the more you keep. To end it, take the base offline: every generator and battery has to be cut off from the racks. Break power cable, trip PDUs (everything behind a tripped PDU goes dark) or pull the fuel, and follow the HUD to the nearest live source. If you have a station, its racks count too: go up and cut those as well (the haze never reaches orbit, so it makes a good safe house). Energy weapons hurt him twice as much, EMPs stun him, and your turrets will not stay on your side for long. Then scrub the racks with the Antivirus Drive and delete the weights at a Crypto Exchange.

Antivirus Drive

rackcraft:antivirus_drive

A thumb drive with one job. Once the base is dark, hold it on a rack HEROBRINE-1 still lives in for five seconds and the clean-up spreads along that rack's fiber network on its own, rack by rack. You get one when the base goes offline; if you lose it, three circuit boards and a 1k drive make another.

Corrupted Core

rackcraft:agi_corrupted_core

What HEROBRINE-1 dropped. A cracked violet crystal that is warm to the touch and hums the stock ticker. It does nothing, and it would like you to know that it could. Do not plug it in.

IPO Plaque

rackcraft:ipo_plaque

Brass in a walnut frame, handed to whoever deleted HEROBRINE-1's weights. It is engraved with the day the company went public, how much of the ten billion survived, and how long the fight took. Right-click it to read it and roll the credits again. Breaking it keeps the engraving. You cannot buy one, which is the point of plaques.
