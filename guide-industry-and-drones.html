Industry and Drones

Once RackCoin stops mattering, power is what's left to spend.

  • Electrolyser (cube, 2x2x2 to 5x5x5, with water against its outside): each core turns an Aluminum Ingot into a Hydrogen Canister every 30 seconds at 4,000 kW. A 3x3x3 draws about 100 MW and a 5x5x5 about 400 MW. Canisters gather in the port. Hydrogen can't be bought at the Exchange.
  • Hydrogen Tank (cube, 2x2x2 to 5x5x5, or 10x10x10 with research; no power; inert until Cryogenic Hydrogen Storage is researched): holds hydrogen as gas, 256 canisters' worth per block and more per block in bigger cubes (40% more in a 5x5x5). An Electrolyser touching a tank, or on the same Item Pipe or Storage Link network, fills it instead of making canisters, with no Aluminum Ingots, and stops only when every tank is full. Storage counts a tank's hydrogen as Hydrogen Canisters and hands it out as them, so Drone Docks, Site Planners, Launch Controls and Assembly Robots draw on the tank through their usual storage connections.
  • Tanker Drone: the one drone that can't be built. The Crypto Exchange sells it for 50,000,000 RC (exchange.tankerDronePrice), and only lists it once Cryogenic Hydrogen Storage is researched. Kept in a Hydrogen Tank's slot (up to 8), each tanker flies a stack of up to 16 canisters at a time, high and fast, to any Drone Dock, Site Planner or Launch Control within 256 blocks whose hydrogen slot isn't full, emptiest first, and comes back for more. Depots on the tank's own Item Pipes are left to storage.
  • Assembly Line: Conveyor Belts carry one item each, a block a second, onto the next belt, into a container, or off the end; items dropped on a belt ride it, and so do players. Robots stand beside a belt facing it, stop any workpiece they can work on in the middle of the belt, and do their step:
RobotStepPower while working
Welding RobotWeld, 6 s1,500 kW
Riveting RobotRivet, 4 s600 kW
Assembly RobotInstall parts from its nine slots, 3 s per step, several steps in a row if they're all installs250 kW

Arms idle at 1 kW, slow down below full power and stop under 10%. A workpiece remembers its progress (its tooltip says what it needs next), so one that falls off half-built can go round again. The first recipe: a Drone Frame gets 4 Electric Motors, a weld, 2 Circuit Boards, a Hydrogen Canister and rivets, and becomes a Maintenance Drone.

  • Drone Dock: holds up to 8 drones, Hydrogen Canisters and spares (rack modules, Repair Kits). Every two seconds it sends a drone to each job within 32 blocks across and 96 up or down (enough for a Wind Tower's nacelle): swapping a Failed Module out of a rack for a spare (the dead one comes back, and a hopper under the dock can take it away), splicing a cut cable with one of a Repair Kit's repairs, resetting a tripped PDU breaker, or servicing a worn Solar Array or Wind Tower. Each trip burns an eighth of a canister; the dock draws 2 kW. On an Item Pipe that reaches storage, a dock also draws drones, hydrogen, Repair Kits and replacement modules from it as needed, and files dead modules away. A Failed Module remembers what it was (its tooltip says so), so a burned-out GPU Blade is replaced with a GPU Blade, never just any spare. Hit a drone and it drops as an item.

Belt Loaders and Belt Unloaders connect a line to storage by Item Pipe: a Loader holds a sample item and puts another from storage onto the belt it faces whenever that belt is empty; an Unloader at the end of the line files whatever arrives back into storage. Neither needs power. A Storage Exporter does the same for any machine or container: on an Item Pipe, facing a Site Planner, Launch Control or chest, it keeps that block topped up to a stack of each of the up to nine sample items in its slots (also no power).

Each belt carries one item, so only one robot works on it at a time; a second robot beside the same belt helps only if it does a different step. Lengthen the line instead. A Satellite Bus can become any of four payloads: the first part an Assembly Robot fits decides which.

An Assembly Robot on an Item Pipe stocks itself: besides topping up the parts already in its slots, it watches the belt in front of it and the five belts feeding it, and when a workpiece's next step is installing a part it has none of, it takes that part from storage into an empty slot (up to 16), buying it if an Exchange Auto-Buyer is on the network and the Exchange sells it. Three R&D projects, Brisk Belts, Frantic Belts and Benny Hill Belts, each double the speed of every Conveyor Belt and every Welding, Riveting and Assembly Robot (2x, 4x, then 8x), for the same power.

Vehicle lines (with Superb Warfare)

With Superb Warfare installed, its ground vehicles (tanks, IFVs, armoured cars and the trucks: everything SBW files under "land" and "civilian") can be built on an Assembly Line instead of at SBW's Vehicle Assembling Table. You need Prime Brokerage.

  • Vehicle Chassis (a welding jig on a pallet): right-click the air to pick the vehicle (sneak to go back through the list), then put it on a Conveyor Belt. It's too big to ride as an item, so it goes on the line as itself: SBW's own model of the vehicle as a pale blue see-through outline, on the jig.
  • It rides the belts from the middle of one to the middle of the next, and stops wherever a robot can do its next step. Robots work on it from up to three blocks to the side of the belt, facing it, so they stand clear of the hull. A chassis waits behind another rather than driving through it.
  • The steps come from SBW's own recipe for the vehicle, in the order a plant would do them:
StageParts (the Bradley's, for example)ThenWhat fills in
Hull8 steel blocks (Rackcraft's or SBW's)weldthe hull
Running gear2 tracks (or wheels)rivettracks and wheels
Powertrainthe motor, the battery packs, and 2 Circuit Boards (Rackcraft's drive computer and fire control)
Armamentthe armament modules, and anything else SBW's recipe asks forweld, rivetthe turret and guns
  • Off the end of the last belt it rolls out as the vehicle, facing down the line, and with Quartermaster installed it comes out charged, loaded and stocked like a dealership vehicle.
  • Right-click a chassis to list what it still needs. Punch it to take it off the line as an item that remembers how far it got.

Rackcraft's steel blocks and ingots count as steel (c:storage_blocks/steel, c:ingots/steel) everywhere, including SBW's own recipes. Operators can try it with /rackcraft vehicleline testsite (the research, chests with a kit, and a working line, at your feet) or /rackcraft kit vehicles.

Line-built upgrades (after Prime Brokerage): Uprated Hulls, Composite Armour, Reactive Plating and Main Battle Spec, at 2M, 6M, 15M and 40M RC. Each tier gives vehicles built on the line another 50% health and battery and 5% less damage, up to three times the stock health and battery and 20% less damage at the top, at whichever tier the building company has reached when the vehicle rolls off. Stock and dealership vehicles stay stock. vehicleLine.upgradePerTier and armourPerTier tune it. With Jade, looking at any Superb Warfare vehicle shows its health and battery, and a line-built one's tier.
