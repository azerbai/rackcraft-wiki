Fuels

Diesel generators are your insurance against outages, and they need fuel. Rackcraft fuels also work in vanilla furnaces.

Coke

rackcraft:coke

Coal or charcoal baked in a blast furnace. It burns twice as long as coal: 160 seconds of diesel runtime.

Crafting
Blast Any coals (10 s)
Crafting
Block of Coke makes 9

Block of Coke

rackcraft:coke_block

Nine pieces of coke pressed into a block. It burns for 1,600 seconds in a Diesel Generator, the longest-lasting fuel in the mod.

Crafting

Biomass Pellet

rackcraft:biomass_pellet

Compressed crop waste. Eight crops, seeds, leaves or saplings plus bone meal make four pellets, which each burn for 40 seconds. It is a renewable fuel for farms.

Biodiesel Canister

rackcraft:biodiesel_canister

Refined biomass in an aluminum can. It runs a Diesel Generator for 8 minutes, the best renewable fuel for backup power.
