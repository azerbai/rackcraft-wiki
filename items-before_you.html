The Company Before You

Spoilers. The Field Manual keeps this chapter for late in the game.

Somebody built these ruins. The chests say so: half the documents in them were written by "Gemerald 3.1 Pro", which is a strange name for a stranger's model, given that it's also yours. Gemerald Cloud's Archive Pages are scattered through what's left of its campus, one story in twelve pieces: look in offices and archives, AI labs and content mills, prototype labs and vaults, and further afield. Read each one, then put them on show in Display Cases, all in one hall, and see what they add up to.

Archive Page

rackcraft:archive_page

A page from Gemerald Cloud's files: memos, logs, a board's minutes. Gemerald Cloud was the company here before you, and the ruins were its campus. Twelve pages are scattered through the ruins (offices and archives, AI labs, content mills, prototype labs, vaults, the Military Base's command room, and one on the Moon). Right-click to read one; your company remembers every page it has read. Put all twelve in Display Cases in one hall and the story pieces itself together.

Display Case

rackcraft:display_case

A walnut plinth under a brass-framed glass case: right-click with anything to put it on show (it turns slowly, label and all), with an empty hand to take it back. A home for souvenirs: the IPO Plaque, a Corrupted Core, a chunk of crater glass, your first Pi Node. Put Gemerald Cloud's twelve Archive Pages in Display Cases in one hall, all within 24 blocks of each other, and the hall pieces the story together. Craft from glass, gold and wood.
