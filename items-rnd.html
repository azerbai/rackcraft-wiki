R&D and the Frontier

Once the cluster is big, the Operations Terminal's R&D tab turns its spare compute and RackCoin into upgrades. Every project costs RackCoin to start and then compute-seconds, which idle racks on Auto clusters work through instead of mining: General projects use general compute, AI projects use AI compute, and the Research share button decides how much of the free compute research may take. Projects come in tiers (faster boots, cheaper power, better sinks, cheaper training, a wider thermal envelope, reactor uprates, and Extreme UV Lithography, which unlocks the Wafer Fab). Repeatables never end: each level costs 2.5 times the RackCoin and twice the compute. Enterprise Sales unlocks Compute Leases: clients rent a share of your AI compute for up to an hour with a 99% or 99.9% uptime guarantee, paying in full only if you keep it (5% less for every 0.1% short). Offers grow with your facility. Frontier runs train ever bigger models on your single biggest Auto cluster, which must keep lending the run's minimum AI compute the whole time: if it drops (an outage, a trip, an overheated hall) the run rolls back to its last checkpoint, every 5%. Pause a run before maintenance and it saves a checkpoint where it stands. The fifth run is an AGI.

Wafer Fab

rackcraft:wafer_fab

A chip fab in a box, or rather in a cube: it etches sixteen Silicon and four GPU Chips into one Wafer-Scale Engine, five minutes per batch per core, 400 kW per working core. It is a multiblock: build a solid cube of 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research) Wafer Fabs. It does nothing until the Extreme UV Lithography research is done at an Operations Terminal, and a 3x3x3 draws over 10 MW while it works, so plan on a reactor array. Feed and empty it from any block, by hand, hopper or Item Pipe.

Wafer-Scale Engine

rackcraft:wafer_scale_engine

The endgame AI module: a whole silicon wafer as one chip. It lends 150 AI compute and 40 general compute (three Quantum Cores' worth of AI) and draws 2 to 16 kW, so a full rack is 128 kW of heat. It can't mine, it is liquid-cooled, and it boots in 20 seconds. It can't be crafted or bought: only a Wafer Fab makes it. Hardware failures never take one out.

Silicon Foundry

rackcraft:silicon_foundry

Melts quartz and sand into silicon by the bucketload: two Nether Quartz and four Sand make eight Silicon, twenty seconds per batch per core, 40 kW per working core. A multiblock: build a solid cube of 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research). Handy for feeding a Wafer Fab, which eats sixteen Silicon an engine. Products gather in the port, the marked corner block.

E-Waste Recycler

rackcraft:ewaste_recycler

Shreds dead hardware back into parts: each Failed Module gives three Silicon and a Copper Wire, fifteen seconds per batch per core, 10 kW per working core. Fried Modules (stripped from flare-fried racks) pay out by tier, up to 16 Silicon and 3 GPU Chips. A multiblock: build a solid cube of 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research). Pairs well with hardware failure events and an Item Pipe. Products gather in the port, the marked corner block.
