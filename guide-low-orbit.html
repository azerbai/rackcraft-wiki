Low Orbit

Orbit is a place: the Low Orbit dimension, with a black sky, hard stars, an unfiltered sun, and the Earth turning below you once an in-game day, dark through orbital night. Each company has its own plot, 8,192 blocks apart, and arrives on a 5 x 5 deck of Station Truss (eight from six Aluminum Ingots and three Steel Ingots). Racks up there pay into the same balance as everywhere else, and mine 75% more RackCoin.

Getting there. Research Crewed Spaceflight (Cryogenic Hydrogen Storage and Personal Propulsion; 40,000,000 RC + 400M general) and build a Return Capsule on the Assembly Line (a Satellite Bus with four Steel Blocks, two Cryo Coils and four Circuit Boards), then send that back down the line for a Crew Capsule (eight Hydrogen Canisters, four Electric Motors and a Respirator). The Crew Capsule is a three-stage payload. Stand on the pad when you press Launch and you ride in its window seat through the count and the climb; about 150 blocks above build height you are in orbit on your company's deck, with a Return Capsule parked beside you. If the launch fails, the escape motor throws you clear under a parachute. Nobody on the pad? The capsule flies empty and waits on the deck.

Coming home. Use the Return Capsule and press [Deorbit] in chat: the screen burns orange with re-entry plasma, then the chute opens high over the pad you left from and you drift down unhurt. The capsule is spent, so keep a spare. Fall off the station instead and you re-enter the hard way: above where you left the Earth, on fire, 12 damage poorer, with Slow Falling for the landing.

Zero gravity. Off the ground in orbit you float. Jump drifts you up and sneak drifts you down, and there is no fall damage. Mag Boots (shapeless: Iron Boots, four Copper Wire, two Steel Ingots and a Circuit Board) put your feet back on the deck whenever there is a block within three below you, so building feels normal; step off the edge and you float again. Riding something or creative flight works as usual. There's no air for wings or rotors, so anything in the rackcraft:air_vehicles entity tag (Superb Warfare's helicopters, planes, airships and drone) won't deploy in orbit; ground vehicles are fine.

Air. Orbit is vacuum, and vacuum doesn't forgive.

  • The suit: outside a pressurised room you breathe from a sealed Pressure Suit: Pressure Helmet, Pressure Suit and Pressure Leggings (each built on the Assembly Line behind Crewed Spaceflight, from a Respirator, an Iron Chestplate and Iron Leggings) with Mag Boots. The helmet holds ten minutes of air and pulls Oxygen Canisters (five minutes each) from your inventory as it empties; the gauge sits over the hunger bar and alarms go at 60, 30 and 10 seconds. With no air at all you get five seconds of NO AIR, then you die, and nothing (armour, Resistance, a totem) saves you. Logging in or arriving gives you ten (or five) seconds before the air starts to count.
  • Oxygen: the Electrolyser makes an Oxygen Canister beside every Hydrogen Canister while its by-product slot has room (otherwise it vents the oxygen and carries on).
  • Rooms: build a room of Pressure Hull and Pressure Glass and put an Oxygen Vent in the wall facing in: with power (2 kW) and canisters it fills up to 4,096 blocks through anything but hull, glass and shut Airlocks. A canister lasts 10 minutes for each 512 blocks. Break the hull and the room vents with a HULL BREACH warning.
  • Airlocks won't open if that would vent a room to space (build two doors and shut one first), unless you sneak to force it. The Crew Capsule won't board anyone without a sealed suit and a minute of air.

Running a station.

  • Heat: there's no air or water to dump heat into, so racks shed it through a coolant loop out to Radiator Panels: 30 kW a face in sunlight, 45 kW in the Earth's shadow, from both broad faces, as long as the 8 blocks out from a face are empty and outside any room. Only Liquid-Immersion Racks run outside a pressurised room.
  • Staying loaded: racks only earn while their chunks are loaded. A Station Core holds the 5 x 5 chunks round itself loaded day and night, through restarts (the 3 x 3 without its 4 kW of power). A company holds at most 49 chunks between all its cores.
  • The network: fiber doesn't reach orbit. A Ground Link on Earth (with open sky above it) and one on the station join the station's fiber to the internet, carrying 100 RC/s like an Uplink Router. Without a link the station has no Ops Terminal, Crypto Exchange, Darknet, Procurement Wall or Auto-Buyer. 6 kW at each end.
  • Freight: pack a Cargo Pod (a 54-slot double chest that rides one Rocket Stage) and launch it: it docks at your company's first free Docking Port in orbit, even with nobody up there, and the port files its cargo into the storage on its fiber. Take the empty pod out to free the port.
  • Debris: a Debris Field now and then punches through a room's outer walls. Whipple Shields seal like Pressure Hull and shrug off three hits, denting each time (an Aluminum Ingot beats a dent out); Debris Trackers in orbit warn you and make it rarer.

Operators can use /rackcraft orbit go and /rackcraft orbit home, and /rackcraft launch start presses Launch on the nearest Launch Control.
