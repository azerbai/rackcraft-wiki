After the Ending: a Public Company

Spoilers. This page covers the end of the game.

Once HEROBRINE-1's weights are deleted you're a listed company, and two things carry on.

Gemerald Cloud's archive. Twelve Archive Pages are spread through the ruins' chests: Site 7, the data-centre office, archive, AI lab, content mill, prototype lab and vault, the Military Base's command room, and one in Nebula Compute's crashed data center on the Moon. Right-click a page to read it like a book; your company remembers every page it has read ("Page 4 of 12 found"). They aren't on the Exchange. The Field Manual's chapter The Company Before You hints where they turn up.

Display Cases (five Glass in a U, over planks, a Gold Ingot and planks) show one item, slowly turning, with its name when you look at it. Right-click with an item to put it in and with an empty hand to take it out. Any item fits, so they double as a trophy shelf for the IPO Plaque. Put all twelve different Archive Pages in cases within 24 blocks of each other and the hall reads the story back, you get the Company Museum challenge, and the Field Manual opens a hidden last chapter.

Quarterly earnings. A quarter is 4 in-game days (about 80 minutes of play). The board sets guidance in RackCoin per second: your mining rate a minute after going public (once HEROBRINE-1's bonuses are out of the numbers; publicCompany.firstGuidanceDelaySeconds), then last quarter's actual plus 5%.

  • Beat it: a dividend of 2% of the quarter's mining income, and the Public Company perk goes up a step, from +10% mining by 2.5 points at a time to +25%.
  • Miss it: the perk drops a step, never below +10%, and nothing is taken.
  • Board mandates: each quarter also brings one odd request, such as half renewable power, every inlet under 30 C, 10 MWh in batteries, 5 AI contracts, 2 research projects, 99.9% uptime, or a rack in orbit, on the Moon or in the snow. Meeting it pays another 1% of the quarter's income and lifts the share price 3%. Missing it costs nothing.
  • The share price (HRBN, opening at 1,337.00) rises 8% on a beat and falls 6% on a miss, and drifts with your run rate in between. The HUD shows it under the RackCoin box.

The Ops Terminal's Company tab (there once you're public) relists the company under a new ticker (1 to 5 letters, for publicCompany.tickerFee, 100M RC by default) and shows the share price, the quarter's run rate against guidance and the time left, the perk, the mandate, the last press release and a bar chart of recent quarters.
