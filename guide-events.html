Events

Events start about three times an hour once a facility has run for a while (events.perHour, events.enabled), and they all do something:

EventWhat happens
Utility outageUtility Intakes supply nothing for 1.5 to 3 minutes
Cooling failureEvery heat sink, CRAC and exhaust fan stops for 2 minutes
Heat waveFor 5 minutes intakes breathe 4 C hotter air, and cooling towers lose 30%. Half as hard in a cold biome, 1.5 times as hard in a hot one (biome temperature under 0.3, or 1 and up), and not at all outside the Overworld
Cold snapFor 5 minutes intakes breathe 6 C cooler air, but cooling towers ice up and lose 40%. Half as strong in a hot biome, 1.5 times as strong in a cold one, and not at all outside the Overworld
Hardware failureOne module per 64 racks (one to three) burns out into a Failed Module, and chat says where. Tier 3 and 4 modules (Quantum Cores, Wafer-Scale Engines and up) are spared, and Predictive Maintenance stops it entirely
Cable cutA random power or fiber cable on a network that feeds a server rack is cut, and the terminal shows where. With no such cable, nothing happens
SurgeEvery rack on a power network without a Battery Bank reboots from cold
Debris FieldIts own, rarer clock (events.debrisPerHour, 0.3 an hour), and only while a Comms Satellite, Orbital Data Center, Dyson Mirror or Strike Platform is in orbit: one of them, picked at random, is knocked out of orbit with a sarcastic chat line. Never two within twenty minutes, never while a strike is counting down. Each Debris Tracker in orbit (up to two) cuts the chance by 75%
