Weapons of Mass Destruction (don't)

For people who have finished everything and want to finish something else. A Strike Platform in orbit and a Targeting Terminal on the ground burn a crater from the sky; a Tactical Warhead and a Burner Phone get a fixer's bomber to drop one. Both refuse targets near spawn, beds and people, both announce themselves to everyone nearby, and both can be switched off in the config (strike.enabled, warhead.enabled). Orbital Defence and Fissile Ordnance in the R&D tab unlock the hardware. Kinetic Artillery unlocks the RC-1 Rail Cannon: a gun you lay a tower for, sit in and aim, and which ruins whatever it is pointed at.

Strike Platform

rackcraft:strike_platform

Bus + a Photonic Tensor Core + 8 Superconducting Wire + 4 Cryo Coils, welded and riveted. Three stages. A capacitor bank and a very large lens, pointed down. Up to three can be in orbit, each needing 10 minutes between shots, and a Targeting Terminal fires them. Needs Orbital Defence.

Assembly Line
Starts from Satellite Bus: 5 steps needs Orbital Defence

Targeting Terminal

rackcraft:targeting_terminal

Fires your Strike Platforms. It needs power and a fiber link to an Uplink or Core Router (the ground station). Type the X and Z twice, press Check to see what is there and who is near it, pick Narrow (radius 24) or Wide (radius 48), and press Arm. The terminal then charges a platform for 60 seconds at 5 MW (less power, slower charge) and everyone within 500 blocks of the target is told to leave. You can cancel for the first 55 seconds. It refuses targets within 128 blocks of world spawn, any player's bed or itself, and anywhere nobody has ever been. Each platform needs 10 minutes between shots. The beam takes everything from the treetops down to a bowl most of a radius deep, punches a lava-bottomed shaft through the middle and burns the rim out to half as far again. Needs Orbital Defence.

Guidance Chip

rackcraft:guidance_chip

A silver- or gold-bin chiplet on a board with a Circuit Board and some Gallium Nitride, riveted: it knows where it is because it knows where it isn't. Built on the Assembly Line. Needs Fissile Ordnance.

Tactical Warhead

rackcraft:tactical_warhead

A Sealed Waste Cask + 4 Enriched Uranium + a Guidance Chip + 4 Steel Blocks, welded and riveted on the Assembly Line. You cannot use it yourself. You need a Burner Phone, a fixer, and a billion RackCoin. Needs Fissile Ordnance.

Assembly Line
Starts from Sealed Waste Cask: 5 steps needs Fissile Ordnance

Strategic Warhead

rackcraft:strategic_warhead

A Tactical Warhead that went to graduate school: 16 more Enriched Uranium, 8 Hydrogen Canisters to boost it, a second Guidance Chip and 8 Steel Blocks, welded and riveted on the Assembly Line. Twice the blast radius of a Tactical (128 blocks, eight times the volume), fallout out to 256 that lasts half as long again, and the fixer wants 5,000,000,000 RC and keeps 416 blocks from anyone. Needs Boosted Fission. Do not look at it when it goes off.

Assembly Line
Starts from Tactical Warhead: 6 steps needs Boosted Fission

Thermonuclear Warhead

rackcraft:thermonuclear_warhead

A Strategic Warhead used as the match for something much worse: 32 Enriched Uranium, 32 Hydrogen Canisters, a Photonic Tensor Core to time it, 16 Steel Blocks and two more Guidance Chips, welded twice and riveted on the Assembly Line. A blast radius of 256 blocks, fallout out to 576 that lasts twice as long, and the whole map will hear it. The fixer wants 25,000,000,000 RC, keeps 736 blocks from anyone, and asks you to stop calling. Needs Thermonuclear Ordnance. Anyone looking at the flash sees nothing else for a while.

Assembly Line
Starts from Strategic Warhead: 8 steps needs Thermonuclear Ordnance

Cobalt Warhead

rackcraft:cobalt_warhead

A Thermonuclear Warhead wrapped in 32 Spent Fuel, with 64 Enriched Uranium, 64 Hydrogen Canisters, 32 Steel Blocks and two more Guidance Chips, welded twice and riveted on the Assembly Line. A blast radius of 512 blocks, and it is salted: fallout out to 1,280 blocks that lasts four times as long as a Tactical's. The fixer wants 125,000,000,000 RC and a very long way from anyone. Needs Salted Ordnance.

Assembly Line
Starts from Thermonuclear Warhead: 8 steps needs Salted Ordnance

Antimatter Warhead

rackcraft:antimatter_warhead

A Cobalt Warhead as the spark for a magnetic trap of antimatter: 32 Cryo Coils, 64 Superconducting Wire, 8 Dimensional Shards and 4 Photonic Tensor Cores, a weld, a Wafer-Scale Engine to hold the field and four more Guidance Chips, welded and riveted on the Assembly Line. A blast radius of 1,024 blocks (the ground is reshaped out to 640 of them), fallout out to 2,048, and a flash seen from the other side of the map. The fixer wants 625,000,000,000 RC. Needs Antimatter Containment. There is nothing bigger.

Assembly Line
Starts from Cobalt Warhead: 10 steps needs Antimatter Containment

Burner Phone

rackcraft:burner_phone

A cheap phone with one number in it. Use it with a Tactical Warhead in your inventory and a Darknet fixer answers in chat: you tell them where to drop it, they want 1,000,000,000 RC and the warhead. Three minutes later a bomber flies over and the target stops existing: a crater about 50 blocks across the radius, a blast out to 64, a floor of glowing radioactive rubble, and fallout out to 96 blocks that eases from Radiation III to I over five in-game days. They will not drop it within 256 blocks of any player, any bed or world spawn, or anywhere nobody has been. Call it off before the drop and they keep 75% of the money. One use.

Warhead Blueprint

rackcraft:warhead_blueprint

A stained set of plans for something nobody should build, found in a Military Base vault. Use it to hand it to R&D: Fissile Ordnance gets half its compute done for free (or, if it has not started, as soon as it does). It looks worried about this.

Glowing Rubble

rackcraft:glowing_rubble

What a Tactical Warhead leaves on the floor of its crater: fused rock that is still hot in the way that does not show up on a thermometer. Standing on it gives Radiation Sickness II. Over a few in-game days (warhead.rubbleDays, while the area is loaded) it cools to Irradiated Rubble, which cools in turn to plain gravel.

Irradiated Rubble

rackcraft:irradiated_rubble

Glowing Rubble that has mostly calmed down. It still gives Radiation Sickness I to anyone standing on it, and it still glows a little at night. Give it a few more days and it is gravel, like nothing ever happened. Something did happen.
