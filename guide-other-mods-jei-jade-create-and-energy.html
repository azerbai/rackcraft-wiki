Other Mods: JEI, Jade, Create and Energy

All optional: Rackcraft plays the same without them.

  • Grid Interface (any energy mod): swaps Rackcraft power with Team Reborn Energy, the "E" most Fabric tech mods use (bundled, so the block always works). Energy pushed into it becomes power on the network it touches, at energy.importEPerKw (3) E/t for each kW. With Crafts & Additions, an Alternator on a Create shaft at full stress (180 E/t from 16,384 SU) gives 60 kW, about 1 kW per 256 SU: a real first power source that doesn't beat diesel. Once Grid Export Permit is researched (10,000 RC and 500k compute, no prerequisites), it also sends power out into anything beside it that takes energy, such as an Electric Motor. That direction is deliberately poor value, because the grid scales to gigawatts: energy.exportEPerKw (0.25, so 4 kW for each E/t, about 15 kW per 256 SU through a motor), at most energy.maxExportPerBlock (240 E/t, one motor at full stress) a block and energy.maxExportPerCompany (1,200 E/t) across a company's interfaces. Each direction holds energy.buffer (5,000 E). An interface with energy waiting to come in doesn't send any out, so it never feeds itself. Create-focused packs can rebalance all of it in the config.
  • Create recipes: with Create installed, Crushing Wheels crush Bauxite Ore (1 Raw Bauxite, 50% a second) and Failed Modules (2 Silicon, 50% a Copper Wire), a Mechanical Press makes 4 Copper Wire from an ingot (a crafting table makes 6), and a heated Basin mixes an iron ingot and coal into Steel. Each is a little worse than the Rackcraft way, but it can be automated.
  • Create Display Links: Rackcraft machines are Display Link sources for display boards, nixie tubes and signs: a terminal's RackCoin Balance, Mining Rate (a rack's own, or the company's at a terminal), the Operations Terminal's Share Price, any machine's Power, and a battery's Battery Charge. Each reads for the machine's owner and refreshes once a second.
  • JEI shows a recipe page for the Assembly Line (the base item, each part in step order with the robot that fits it, the welds and rivets, the time and the research it needs; a diced wafer's page shows the silicon lottery's odds) and for each processing machine: the Uranium Mill, Gas Centrifuge, Fuel Fabricator, Cask Sealer, Wafer Fab, Silicon Foundry, E-Waste Recycler, Electrolyser, CVD Furnace, Epitaxy Reactor, Regolith Sifter and Isotope Separator, with their time and power per core. "Uses" works on every part. Crafting-table recipes were already in JEI. Items with no recipe at all (ores, Moon soil, ruin finds and loot, AI training data, the ending's keepsakes, creative machines) get an information page with their Field Manual entry, which says where they come from. Vehicle line recipes are read from Superb Warfare when a world starts, so they only show in singleplayer and for the host of a LAN world.
  • Jade shows what a Multimeter would tell you when you look at a machine: a rack's status, temperatures, draw and heat, a reactor's array, output and fuel, a battery's charge, and so on, plus who owns it. Hold Shift for its coolant loop and power network. Cables show their paint, whether they're cut or waiting on research, a Trunk Bundle's lanes, and with Shift the power network or coolant loop they carry. The fiber line and the Multimeter's machine counts stay on the Multimeter only, because they walk the whole network.
