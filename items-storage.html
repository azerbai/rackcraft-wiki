Storage & Autocrafting

Store items on drives in Storage Arrays, cold-archive them on tape, and reach it all from a Storage Terminal on the same fiber. Autocrafting runs on your server racks: they lend compute (Pi 1, 1U 2, GPU 6, Quantum 20, ASIC none, and the specialists: Crafting Coprocessor 8, Crafting Accelerator 30) and stop mining while a job runs. In the terminal, middle-click a craftable item (marked +) to order more, even when some is in stock.

Storage Array

rackcraft:storage_array

Eight bays for Storage Drives. Like a rack it needs power (0.4 kW plus 0.15 kW per drive), takes cool air in the front and exhausts heat out the back. Link it to a Storage Terminal with Fiber Cable. If it loses power or reaches 40 C, its drives go offline until fixed; nothing is lost.

Tape Library

rackcraft:tape_library

Cold storage. Holds four Tape Cartridges of a million items each and draws only 0.3 kW. Items on tape take two seconds to mount before they reach your inventory. When your drives pass 85% full, the items you have not used for longest are archived to tape automatically.

Storage Terminal

rackcraft:storage_terminal

Your window into storage on the same fiber network. Search, take and deposit items; craft on the 3x3 grid, which refills itself from storage; encode Recipe Patterns; and start autocrafting jobs. Ctrl-click a craftable item (marked +) to choose how many to make.

Item Pipe

rackcraft:item_pipe

Moves items between storage and the machines that use them. Connect Storage Arrays or Tape Libraries to Kids' Art Tables, Scriptorium Desks, Diesel Generators and Modular Reactors: once a second they are topped up from storage (paper, crayons, ink, fuel, Fuel Cells) and finished Art Aggregates and Text Corpora go back in. It is its own network, separate from fiber.

Storage Link

rackcraft:storage_link

Joins storage without another cable. Every powered Storage Link in a dimension is linked to every other, and all the storage beside them becomes one network: put a link against (or on the fiber of) each Storage Array cluster, and every terminal, Wireless Terminal, Item Pipe and machine reaching any link sees all of it. A machine touching a link (a Site Planner, a Drone Dock, a Launch Control) draws from the whole lot with no pipe at all. Draws 4 kW; it must be powered, which it can be just by touching a powered machine.

Crafting
Ender eye

Exchange Auto-Buyer

rackcraft:auto_buyer

The company credit card, in a box. Put it on a storage network (touching a Storage Array, a Storage Link, an Item Pipe or a machine that restocks from storage) and power it, and whenever a machine on that storage restocks and storage comes up short, it buys the difference at Crypto Exchange prices out of the RackCoin balance: generator fuel, Fuel Cells, process inputs, paper and ink, Repair Kits and replacement modules, Assembly Robot parts, site materials. A Diesel Generator with no fuel in storage gets the best fuel the Exchange sells. It can't buy what the Exchange doesn't sell: drones, hydrogen and Assembly Line products. Its screen shows what it has spent. Draws 2 kW.

Recipe Pattern

rackcraft:recipe_pattern

An encoded crafting recipe. Store it anywhere in the network and its output becomes autocraftable; the planner chains patterns together for multi-step recipes.

Wireless Transmitter

rackcraft:wireless_transmitter

Lets a Wireless Terminal reach this fiber network. Range starts at 16 blocks. Upgrade it in its screen with RackCoin or with resources: up to 1,024 blocks, then unlimited in this dimension, then every dimension. Each level doubles its power draw.

Ender pearl

Wireless Terminal

rackcraft:wireless_terminal

A Storage Terminal in your pocket. Sneak-right-click a Wireless Transmitter to link it, then right-click anywhere in range. Beyond its range or in another dimension it needs an upgraded transmitter.
