Arms and Ruins

The only places that fight back are the Military Base and the hostile data centers. Nothing roams, nothing raids, nothing ambushes you at home: if you never go looking, nobody minds. Weapons are not for sale at the Exchange; the Directed Energy and Plasma Weapons projects (R&D tab) unlock using them, and anything you loot is inert until then.

Weapons. Each has a heat meter (shown on the tooltip) that cools by itself and locks the weapon out for four seconds with a hiss at 100%. A Coolant Pack carried in your inventory vents every weapon ten times faster; each shot while it is warm wears the pack down a notch. Ammo comes from your inventory or a Wireless Terminal's storage.

End Crystals. Every weapon sets them off: the flame jet, an arc (when the crystal is the first thing in its path), a rail slug or a lance beam passing through one, or a plasma bolt landing near one.

Mk II refits. Once Mil-Spec Refits is researched, put a weapon on an Assembly Line and it comes off as the same weapon, Mk II, with its ammo and heat kept. It shimmers in the inventory and has "Mk II" in its name. Every Mk II has half again the range and damage, gets twice the shots out of each canister and cell, and builds heat half as fast. The Arc Coil also chains two more links, the Railgun pierces two more blocks and the Plasma Rifle splashes wider. The Flamethrower Mk II is oxygen-fed and much worse to be in front of: an 18-block jet, faster and fatter, with three and a half times the damage. It splashes burning fuel on everything near where it lands, sets the floor alight where its fuel comes down, and keeps victims burning twice as long. Both jets fly as their flames do: fast out of the nozzle, slowing and drooping a little as they go, and what burns (mobs, floors, walls) is what the flames you see actually reach. The multipliers are in the weapons config (refitRange, refitDamage, refitAmmo, refitHeat, refitFlameDamage, refitFlameRange).

Shelved until 3.0: the RC-1 is being rebuilt with a new model, effects and damage, and what follows describes the old version. It can't be built for now: its recipes, research, quest and Field Manual pages are gone. A cannon already standing stays where it is and won't seat anyone, and breaking it gives it back as an item. Rounds you own and Kinetic Artillery, if you finished it, are kept for 3.0.

The RC-1 Rail Cannon (Kinetic Artillery) is one gun, 55 blocks from the back of its turret to the muzzle, that you sit in and aim. An Assembly Line builds it from a stock Railgun: 2 Lithium-Ion Battery Banks, 16 Superconducting Wire, 12 Steel Blocks, a weld, 4 Photonic Chips, a CDU, another weld, rivets. It must stand dead centre on a tower you lay yourself: a solid 5x5 column of Gun Emplacement blocks (eight from a Steel Block ringed with Gray Concrete) at least 12 high. Run a power cable up to the cannon; it draws 25 MW for ten seconds to fill its capacitors, and lights up when they are full.

Right-click it to climb into the cupola. The turret and barrel follow your look through a periscope sight that shows the loaded round, how many are left, and the capacitor and charge bars. Swap hands (F) picks AP or HE. Hold use to charge: the ten coils light one after another down the barrel over three seconds, then let go to fire a beam that holds for two and a half seconds along the line it fired on. The shot is heard across the map (a crack and a ringing barrel close up, a rolling boom far off), cracks past anyone near its path, blows a ring of dust out round the tower's foot, and knocks down anyone stood within 12 blocks of the muzzle. Sneak to climb out.

Each shot spends one round, built on the Assembly Line from a Sabot Casing (4 Steel Blocks, 2 Graphene Sheets, 2 Superconducting Wire and a Battery Cell), and each round is 64 Steel Blocks plus more:

  • AP (4 Superconducting Wire, 2 Photonic Chips, a gold Chiplet): bores a 7-wide tunnel through up to 160 blocks of ground, does 1,000 damage to everything within two blocks of its line, and blows a crater 20 blocks across where it finally stops.
  • HE (16 Hydrogen Canisters, a Guidance Chip, 4 Graphene Sheets): stops at the first block or mob it meets and detonates in a fireball, throwing the ground into the air, blowing a ragged hole about 56 blocks across round where it hit (a bowl in flat ground, a cave in a cliff), flattening plants, wood, glass and dirt out to 60 blocks from the hit, and doing up to 800 damage within 40 blocks (and throwing and burning what it doesn't kill). The hole is carved outward over a second or so, like a warhead's.

Both set off End Crystals. What the beam may break is set by weapons.cannonSparesBuilds (default on: Rackcraft blocks, chests, obsidian and bedrock are spared; off, only bedrock and obsidian), and weapons.cannonBreaksBlocks turns world damage off entirely. Every other number is in the weapons config (cannon*).

Sentry Turrets (Laser, Arc and Railgun variants) are powered machines: 1.5 kW, a 16-block reach, and a target scan twice a second. They shoot hostile mobs and angry guards, never you, pets, villagers or drones. Use one to cycle all hostile / guards only / off. A turret heats as it fires and cools only with a coolant loop that has a tower or chiller on it, so an uncooled one overheats within seconds and stands down until it has cooled.

Guards (Soldiers, Security Robots, Scavengers) follow zombified-piglin rules. On Peaceful they ignore you unless you hit one, and then it and its neighbours come for you for a few minutes. On Easy and Normal they act when you come within 12 blocks with a line of sight (8 for the jittery Scavengers); on Hard they open fire on sight from 20 (16). They shoot visible, slow bolts you can sidestep, stay at their posts, never spawn on their own and stand down and heal once they have lost you for 30 seconds. Robots are the ones an EMP can freeze.

The Military Base is a 90 x 90 fenced compound: barracks, a motor pool, an armoury, a command bunker with a vault, six laser turrets, around twenty guards and a named commander. Three ways in: fight, cut the power or EMP it. The turrets and the Blast Door gate run off a generator shed outside the south wall and one cable into the fenced ring; a Blast Door stays shut only while a cable touching it leads back to a live source, so breaking the cable outside the wall, destroying the generator or throwing an EMP opens the gate (and kills the turrets, but not the soldiers). The vault door is on its own power inside the bunker. The vault holds the best loot in the mod, scarce on purpose: Gold chiplets, the odd Wafer-Scale Engine, Superconducting Wire, Battery Cells, EMP Grenades, a Warhead Blueprint and a random weapon. Look for the Mission Log in the command bunker.

Hostile data centers are ordinary ruins with Scavengers in them (and, one time in three, a named boss), and a stash of components behind them.

Bounties. Name an elite by finding one (any elite within 64 blocks of you) and the Darknet posts a bounty on it, shown among the Operations Terminal's alerts with where it was last seen; killing it pays 250,000 RC once.
