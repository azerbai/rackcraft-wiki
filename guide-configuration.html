Configuration

config/rackcraft.json is written on first launch. Main keys include sim.stepTicks (10), thermal.ambientC (24), thermal.cellCapacityKjPerK (4), thermal.faceConductanceKwPerK (2), thermal.upwardMultiplier (2), thermal.leakKwPerK (0.005, through walls), thermal.outdoorLeakKwPerK (0.4, under open sky), thermal.maxActiveCells (65536), thermal.settleEpsilonK (0.03), events.enabled, events.perHour (3), and heatOverlay.maxCells (2000). Config files from before the air model was rebuilt (no version, or below 2) get the new thermal defaults once.

Weapons, turrets and guards have their own weapons block: cooling and lockout (coolPerSecond, coolPackPerSecond, lockoutTicks), every weapon's damage, ammo and heat, the EMP radius and seconds, the sentry range, damage and draw, each guard's health and engage range by difficulty, and the bounty payout. weapons.blockDamage turns scorching and melting off, and weapons.meltGlass lets the Lance Laser melt glass.

The destructive endgame is switchable: launch.destructiveFailures (true) and launch.failureBlastScale (1); strike.enabled, cooldownMinutes (10), narrowRadius (24), wideRadius (48), chargeMw (5), countdownSeconds (60), safeRadius (128); warhead.enabled, priceRc (1,000,000,000), cancelRefund (0.25), deliverySeconds (180), blastRadius (64), falloutRadius (96), falloutDays (5), rubbleDays (6), safeRadius (256); and events.debrisPerHour (0.3). Config files from before version 4 get the new strike and warhead defaults once. The Exchange's tiers and surge are under exchange: tierTwoPremium (5), surgePercent (3), materialSurgePercent (1) and surgeHalfLifeTicks (4,000).

The ending's numbers are under ending:

  • Hiccups: hiccups (true), hiccupMinutes (15), hiccupMinMinutes (3), ominousHours (3).
  • IPO and drain: ipoPayout (10,000,000,000), ipoDelaySeconds (60), drainMinutes (20), enragedDrainFactor (1.5).
  • Haze: outputMin/outputMax (0.5/1.5), outputRerollSeconds (5), exhaustFactor (1.5).
  • Arrival: arrivalRadius (48), hazeMinSeconds (60).
  • Boss: bossHealth (1,000), healthPerExtraPlayer (0.6), copyHealthShare (0.4), copyRespawnSeconds (20), leashBlocks (200), energyWeaponFactor (2), empStunSeconds (5), meleeDamage (7), boltDamage (5), healOnPlayerDeath (0.05).
  • Cutscene and scrub: cutsceneSeconds (3), scrubSeconds (5).
  • Switches: keepInventory, blackout, fakeCrash and music (all true).
  • After the weights: publicCompanyMining (1.1), and the whispers: whispers (true), whisperMinutesMin/whisperMinutesMax (30/60).

The public company's numbers are under publicCompany: quarterDays (4), guidanceGrowth (0.05), dividendShare (0.02), perkStepPercent (2.5), perkMaxPercent (25), beatPrice/missPrice (0.08/0.06), and mandateBonusShare/mandatePrice (0.01/0.03).
