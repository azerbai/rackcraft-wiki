Power

Every machine touching the same Power Cable run, or touching another power machine, is on one network. Each network draws on its sources in a fixed order: power beamed down from orbit first, then solar, wind, reactors and the utility feed, then batteries, then diesel. Surplus charges batteries. When power runs short, cooling and other machines are fed before racks. Battery Banks built into a solid cube (2x2x2 up to 5x5x5, or 10x10x10 with research) become one Grid-Scale Battery, bigger and less lossy per bank. The bigger Solar Arrays and Wind Towers come off the Assembly Line: see Wind and Solar at Scale.

Power Cable

rackcraft:power_cable

Links generators, batteries, PDUs and loads into one power network. Every machine touching the same connected cable run shares power. Cable cut events sever a segment; right-click it with a Repair Kit to splice it.

Solar Panel

rackcraft:solar_panel

Produces up to 4 kW in daylight when the block above it can see the sky. Output scales with sky light. A solar panel is the first source drawn on in any network.

Wind Turbine

rackcraft:wind_turbine

Produces up to 30 kW, depending on altitude and the wind. It runs at 25% at Y 70 or below and reaches full output at Y 130; thunderstorms add another 50%. The wind itself rises and falls by up to a quarter over a few minutes, the same for every turbine, so a battery smooths a wind farm nicely. It needs no fuel and no upkeep. For far more, build a Wind Tower.

Battery Bank

rackcraft:battery_bank

Stores 3,000 kJ of surplus power, charging at up to 15 kW and discharging at up to 60 kW; it loses 10% of the energy going in and 10% coming out. Build Battery Banks into a solid cube, 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research), and they form a Grid-Scale Battery: one store holding every bank's charge and charging and discharging at every bank's rate, with 10% more capacity per bank for each step up in size (40% more in a 5-cube) and smaller losses, 8% each way for a 2-cube down to 2% for a 5-cube.

Lithium-Ion Battery Bank

rackcraft:lithium_battery_bank

A Battery Bank rebuilt on the Assembly Line with eight Battery Cells (Grid Storage Chemistry). It stores 30,000 kJ, ten times a Battery Bank, and charges at 150 kW and discharges at 500 kW. Lithium-Ion banks build into their own Grid-Scale Battery cubes, with the same size bonuses and smaller losses as the plain kind; a 5x5x5 holds over 5 GJ, enough to carry a megawatt through a night several times over. Please do not puncture.

Assembly Line
Starts from Battery Bank: 4 steps needs Grid Storage Chemistry

Solid-State Battery Bank

rackcraft:solid_state_battery

The last word in batteries: a Lithium-Ion bank rebuilt with graphene, gallium nitride and superconducting wire (Solid-State Cells). 250,000 kJ per bank, charging at 1,200 kW and discharging at 4,000 kW, so a cube of them soaks up a whole solar farm's afternoon and gives it back to a data hall all night. It forms its own Grid-Scale Battery cubes.

Assembly Line
Starts from Lithium-Ion Battery Bank: 5 steps needs Solid-State Cells

Diesel Generator

rackcraft:diesel_generator

Backup generator that delivers 40 kW. It spins up only when demand exceeds solar, wind, reactor and utility supply, and takes 10 seconds to get there. It burns any furnace fuel plus Rackcraft fuels from its slot, only while it is actually supplying power. Open it to watch its output, fuel and spin-up live; it smokes while running and vents 4 kW of heat out of its radiator at the back.

Gas Turbine

rackcraft:gas_turbine

A Diesel Generator sent down the Assembly Line for motors, steel and controls (Combined-Cycle Turbines). It makes 400 kW, ten times a diesel, burning the same fuels eight times as fast, so each lump of coal goes a quarter further. Like the diesel, it spins up only when demand outruns solar, wind, reactor and utility supply, takes ten seconds to get there, and vents a tenth of its output as heat out of its radiator. Item Pipes keep it fed from storage.

Assembly Line
Starts from Diesel Generator: 5 steps needs Combined-Cycle Turbines

Grid Interface

rackcraft:grid_interface

Swaps power with other mods' energy (Team Reborn Energy, the E most tech mods use). Energy pushed into it becomes Rackcraft power on the network it touches, at 3 E/t for each kW, so a Crafts & Additions Alternator on a Create shaft is a fine first power source. Once Grid Export Permit is researched it also sends power the other way, into anything beside it that takes energy, such as an Electric Motor. That direction is deliberately poor value: 4 kW for each 1 E/t, capped at 240 E/t a block and 1,200 E/t a company, because the grid scales and Create shouldn't spin forever off a fusion ring.
