Nuclear

The full fuel cycle, and every machine in it is a multiblock: a solid cube of the same machine, 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research), and a serious power draw. Mine Uranium Ore deep underground; a Uranium Mill turns two Raw Uranium into a Yellowcake; a Gas Centrifuge enriches six Yellowcake into one Enriched Uranium and four Depleted Uranium; a Fuel Fabricator makes one Fuel Cell from each Enriched Uranium and two Steel Ingots; Modular Reactors burn them and leave Spent Fuel, which stops the reactor once its waste slots fill up. Spent Fuel is radioactive in your pockets: a Cask Sealer seals four of them with four Depleted Uranium into a harmless Waste Cask. Each cube's products gather in its port, the bottom corner framed in cyan: open it and press Collect all. A cube short of power runs slower rather than stopping. Connect every cube to a Storage Array with Item Pipe and the whole cycle runs by itself.

Uranium Ore

rackcraft:uranium_ore

Faintly glowing ore found deep down, from Y -64 to Y 16, in small veins. Mine it with an iron pickaxe for Raw Uranium (Fortune helps, Silk Touch keeps the ore). Raw Uranium can't be smelted: it goes to a Uranium Mill, two to a Yellowcake, and a Fuel Cell takes twelve.

Raw Uranium

rackcraft:raw_uranium

Uranium ore straight out of the ground. Feed it to a Uranium Mill to make Yellowcake.

Uranium Mill

rackcraft:uranium_mill

Grinds and leaches Raw Uranium into Yellowcake: two ore make one. Like every nuclear machine it is a multiblock: build Uranium Mills into a solid cube, 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research), to make it work at all. Every block in the cube is a core: each core finishes one batch every 30 seconds and draws 20 kW while working, and bigger cubes use up to 20% less power per core. Inputs are shared evenly across the cube; feed and empty it from any block, by hand, hopper or Item Pipe.

Gas Centrifuge

rackcraft:gas_centrifuge

Enriches uranium: spins six Yellowcake into one Enriched Uranium and four Depleted Uranium, two minutes per batch per core, 60 kW per working core. A multiblock: build a solid cube of 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research) Gas Centrifuges (a real cascade, and a real power bill).

Fuel Fabricator

rackcraft:fuel_fabricator

The only way to make Fuel Cells: one Enriched Uranium and two Steel Ingots make one cell, a minute per batch per core, 30 kW per working core. A multiblock: build a solid cube of 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research).

Fuel Cell

rackcraft:fuel_cell

The fuel for the Modular Reactor: one cell runs a reactor for 30 minutes at full output and leaves a Spent Fuel rod behind. It can't be crafted or bought: a Fuel Fabricator makes it from Enriched Uranium. Item Pipes can deliver them from storage.

Modular Reactor

rackcraft:modular_reactor

A small modular reactor: 500 kW of steady power, burning one Fuel Cell every 30 minutes at full output (less at part load), and every spent cell comes out as Spent Fuel in its waste slot. Build reactors into a solid cube, 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research), and they form one reactor array: the casing changes to show it, the output of every core adds up, fuel and waste are shared evenly across every core's slots, and each core burns 5% (2-cube) up to 20% (5-cube) less fuel. When the waste slots are full the reactor stops: seal the Spent Fuel into casks. Its heat, 30% of its output, goes into its coolant loop if piped, otherwise into the air.

Spent Fuel

rackcraft:spent_fuel

A burnt-out Fuel Cell, and very much still radioactive. Carrying it gives you Radiation Sickness, worse the more you carry. Pipe it out of the reactor into storage and on to a Cask Sealer, which seals four of them into a Waste Cask.

Cask Sealer

rackcraft:cask_sealer

Seals nuclear waste away for good: four Spent Fuel and four Depleted Uranium (the shielding) make one Sealed Waste Cask, a minute per batch per core, 15 kW per working core. A multiblock: build a solid cube of 2x2x2 up to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research).

Sealed Waste Cask

rackcraft:waste_cask

Four Spent Fuel rods sealed behind steel and depleted uranium. Completely safe to carry, stack and store: line them up on a concrete pad somewhere and try not to think about the next ten thousand years.
