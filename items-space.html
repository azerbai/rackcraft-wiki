The Launch Programme

Hydrogen's real job. Build Rocket Stages and payloads on the Assembly Line, fill a Launch Control's tank with Hydrogen Canisters, and put things in orbit. Comms Satellites speed up the Darknet and add lease slots, Survey Satellites find a Hyperscale Campus for you, Orbital Data Centers add compute to every rack, and Dyson Mirrors beam power back down to Rectennas. The last two have no limit. Space is a mess, though: a Debris Field now and then knocks one thing out of orbit, and Debris Trackers make that rarer. A launch that fails comes down on the pad, so build the spaceport away from home. With Crewed Spaceflight you can go up yourself: see Life in Orbit.

Launch Control

rackcraft:launch_control

Runs the launch programme. Place it touching a 3x3 Launch Pad. Load Rocket Stages into its first three slots, a payload into the fourth, and Hydrogen Canisters into the fifth (hoppers and Item Pipes work): canisters are drained into its tank, which holds 4,096. Each stage burns 128 canisters. Comms and Survey Satellites and Debris Trackers need one stage, an Orbital Data Center two, a Dyson Mirror or Strike Platform three (a Saturn V, nearly five blocks across and sixty tall: it needs a 5x5 of open sky over the pad). Press Launch, and after a ten-second countdown the rocket lifts off. About one launch in fifty ends in a rapid unscheduled disassembly: the stages and fuel are lost, the payload's abort motor brings it back, and the wreck comes down on the pad in a real explosion (bigger rockets and fuller tanks make bigger ones). It destroys the pad and anything plain nearby; Rackcraft machines and cables survive. Build the spaceport away from home. On the Moon it only flies Cargo Pods, and only home: one stage, a Heat Shield in a stage slot, and a free Docking Port in the Overworld. The stages and hydrogen have to be on the Moon already. Draws 5 kW, and needs power to launch.

Satellite Bus

rackcraft:satellite_bus

The chassis every payload is built on. What it becomes depends on the first part an Assembly Robot fits: copper wire makes a Comms Satellite, a Thermal Scanner a Survey Satellite, Wafer-Scale Engines an Orbital Data Center, Solar Panels a Dyson Mirror, Spyglasses a Debris Tracker, a Photonic Tensor Core a Strike Platform.

Comms Satellite

rackcraft:comms_satellite

Bus + 16 Copper Wire + 4 Circuit Boards, welded and riveted. One stage. In orbit, each one (up to five) makes Darknet parcels arrive 10% sooner and adds a Compute Lease slot.

Assembly Line
Starts from Satellite Bus: 4 steps

Survey Satellite

rackcraft:survey_satellite

Bus + a Thermal Scanner + 2 Circuit Boards, riveted. One stage. It photographs the world once and drops the results into the Launch Control: a map marking the nearest Hyperscale Campus nobody has found yet (or, failing that, the nearest abandoned data center).

Assembly Line
Starts from Satellite Bus: 3 steps

Orbital Data Center

rackcraft:orbital_datacenter

Bus + 4 Wafer-Scale Engines + 4 Cryo Coils, welded and riveted. Two stages. Up there the sun never sets and space is cold: every module in orbit adds 3% to the AI and general compute of every rack you own. There is no limit, and repeatable research never stops wanting more.

Assembly Line
Starts from Satellite Bus: 4 steps

Dyson Mirror

rackcraft:dyson_mirror

Bus + 16 Solar Panels, welded and riveted. Three stages. One more mirror in the swarm around the sun: each beams 2 MW down to your Rectennas, forever. It takes a lot of hydrogen to put one up and a long time to pay it back, which is rather the point of a Dyson swarm.

Assembly Line
Starts from Satellite Bus: 3 steps

Debris Tracker

rackcraft:debris_tracker

Bus + 2 Spyglasses + 2 Circuit Boards, riveted. One stage. A small satellite that watches the junk up there and nudges everything else out of its way: each one in orbit cuts the chance of a Debris Field hit by 75%, for up to two. With one up, a piece heading for your station is announced thirty seconds out, with where it will hit.

Assembly Line
Starts from Satellite Bus: 3 steps

Rectenna

rackcraft:rectenna

Catches the power your Dyson Mirrors beam down from orbit. Each mirror sends 2 MW, shared out between every Rectenna in the dimension that can see the sky, up to 20 MW per Rectenna. It works day and night, never runs out and can't be sold through a Grid-Tie Substation. Without mirrors in orbit it does nothing.
