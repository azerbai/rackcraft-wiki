The Ending

Spoilers. This page covers the end of the game.

The last project in the R&D tab is Go Public (1,000,000,000 RC and 3e10 compute-seconds, half AI and half general, after HEROBRINE-1). It asks twice before it starts, because going public has consequences. Your builds and your RackCoin are safe. The Field Manual's chapter on it appears when you need it.

<details> <summary>Spoilers: how the ending works</summary>

  • Before the IPO: once HEROBRINE-1 is awake he has small, harmless hiccups: chat lines with "typos" that read like threats, cosmetic contract notes, and research statuses on the Ops Terminal that rewrite themselves ("I'm fine."). They come more often the longer you wait, from every 15 minutes down to every 3. ending.hiccups turns them off.
  • The IPO: the bell rings, a banking screen shows ten billion RC arriving in Personal Funds, a balance that only exists for the ending, and the HUD counts it in with coins and confetti. A minute later he goes wrong.
  • The haze: a purple vignette and glitches over the machines. Every few seconds each rack's mining is multiplied by a random factor from 0.5 to 1.5, and rack exhaust runs 50% hotter (intake is never touched). He starts draining Personal Funds at once: all of it in 20 minutes, faster in his last phase. He materialises once you reach the base (the rack cluster nearest you when the haze began; the HUD points the way).
  • The fight: HEROBRINE-1 has 1,000 health, +60% for each other player within 128 blocks. He works on every difficulty, Peaceful included, and stays within 200 blocks of the base; he heals slowly if everyone leaves. Energy weapons (Plasma Rifle, Lance Laser, Arc Coil) do double damage, and an EMP stuns him for 5 seconds.
  • In an armed world (Superb Warfare or TaCZ installed), a tank or a gun emplacement made him a formality, so he comes armed too: 5 times the health, 2.5 times the damage, no single hit takes more than 150 of his health (before the energy-weapon bonus), and every 15 seconds (10 in his last third) he hijacks the vehicle or emplacement his target is in, throwing everyone out and doing 60 damage to it. Getting back in is allowed. Under ending: armedWorld (true; false turns all of it off), armedHealthFactor, armedDamageFactor, armedMaxHit, armedHijackSeconds, armedHijackDamage.
    • Phase one: bolts and short teleports. Your turrets fight for you.
    • Phase two: glitch drones, a pulse that trips every PDU nearby, a blackout, nearby Maintenance and Construction Drones hijacked to dive at you (hit one to snap it out of it), and every powered turret turns on you until you cut its power.
    • Phase three: faster, with one nasty joke.
    • Players keep their inventories until the base goes dark, and every death heals him 5%.
  • The shutdown: when he dies the camera turns to the racks (Esc lets go) and two weaker copies walk out. Take the base offline: every generator and battery must be cut off from every powered rack. Breaking cable, tripping a PDU (everything behind it goes dark) and pulling the fuel all count. The HUD counts the live sources and points at the nearest one. The copies don't drain money, but while the base has power you can't get rid of them: kill one and another reboots out of the racks 20 seconds later (ending.copyRespawnSeconds). Every powered turret stays his until the end. If you die, the copies reset. When the last source goes dark, what is left of Personal Funds goes into your RackCoin balance and the credits roll: a title card, the lines to their own score, your numbers (Personal Funds saved, fight time, racks) before the sign-off, and a moment, partway through, when something breaks in. Edit them in config/rackcraft_credits.txt: the first heading is the title card, lines starting with # are headings, and the last line is held on its own at the end.
  • The scrub: you get an Antivirus Drive (three circuit boards and a 1k drive make another). Hold it on an infected rack for five seconds and the clean-up spreads along that rack's fiber network. Racks in unloaded chunks are treated as clean.
  • The weights: take HEROBRINE-1's weights (a fresh copy turns up in the outbox if you've lost them) to a Crypto Exchange and right-click twice. His +50% mining and +25% compute are gone for good. The Public Company's shareholder buybacks pay +10% mining instead (ending.publicCompanyMining). You get an IPO Plaque engraved with the date, the Personal Funds you saved and how long the fight took; right-click it to roll the credits again. Afterwards, rarely (every 30 to 60 minutes, ending.whispers), something in the racks still says a line in chat. Everything HEROBRINE-1 says in chat comes from data/rackcraft/herobrine/ (over 300 lines a stage: his jokes once awake, the run-up to the IPO, which turns from slips to plain menace over ending.ominousHours (3), taunts through each act of the ending, and the whispers after).
  • In orbit: the ending runs from the Overworld, but your station is part of it. If you're up there when the bell rings, HEROBRINE-1 takes the station's Airlocks for a minute and talks over the station speaker (sneaking still forces a door, so nobody is shut in without air). The haze doesn't reach orbit and the station's turrets stay yours, so it's a safe house, but its racks count toward the shutdown: if the base is dark and the station still feeds racks, he says so and the HUD counts the station's sources (and points at them once you're up there). Go up and cut those too. The Moon is left out of all this.

Nothing in the ending removes a block or any RackCoin, and you rewire the base yourself afterwards. Every number is in the ending section of the config, and /rackcraft ending ... can inspect, skip, restart or abort it. </details>
