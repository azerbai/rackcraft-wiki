Getting Around

  • Grapple: use it to hook the block you are looking at, up to 40 blocks away, and be pulled to it, faster as you go. Sneak to let go. One Battery Cell a shot, and the end of the pull is cushioned. The rope is a line of grey sparks.
  • Hydrogen Jetpack (chest slot, Personal Propulsion): hold jump to climb, sneak in the air to hover, sprint while climbing to boost. Thirty seconds of thrust per Hydrogen Canister (hovering costs half), taken from the canisters you carry or a Wireless Terminal's storage in reach. Run dry in a long fall and it vents the last of its gas once, every five minutes, for a slow fall. The exhaust is real: anything standing in the 3.5 blocks under a thrusting pack is set alight and takes 3 damage per hit (2 while hovering), credited to the pilot. The pack's flame is particles; a Fuel Cell version was not built.
  • Mag-Lev (Magnetic Levitation): Rails and Stations that touch form a line. A rail is also a power-network block, so it carries power from any cable it touches; a cut rail breaks the line (a Repair Kit mends it). Right-click a Station to ride to its destination (the nearest until you choose), sneak-right-click with an empty hand to cycle destinations down the line. A car carries one rider at up to 54 blocks a second, draws 800 kW from its starting Station's network while it runs, slows with the share the grid can give and stalls under 10%, and stops at a missing or cut rail.
  • Quantum Teleport Pads (Quantum Entanglement): stand on a pad for two seconds and arrive on the pad it is entangled with. Use a Linked Shard on one pad and then another to entangle them (the shard is used up); a Dimensional Shard (a Linked Shard, a Nether Star and an Eye of Ender) pairs pads in different dimensions at twenty times the cost; sneak-use cuts a pair. Each pad needs a Quantum Annealer in its slot and a Cryostat touching it. A jump burns a Hydrogen Canister from the source's Cryostat and a burst of power from its network, a megawatt-second per 100 blocks (at least 100), so the grid has to be able to answer; the pad's screen shows the cost. The pad you land on needs only its Annealer and a stocked Cryostat, and room above it. You don't bounce back when you land. None of it is for sale at the Exchange.

Tunable under building in config/rackcraft.json: teleportMwsPer100Blocks and teleportCanisters.
