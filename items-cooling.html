Cooling

Every kilowatt a rack draws comes back out as heat, and a rack slows down once its intake air passes 27 C and stops at 40 C. Heat leaves a rack two ways. AIR: out of the back into the room, where it spreads and slowly leaks through the walls (outdoors it blows away much faster). Keep it off the intakes with hot and cold aisles, blanking panels and raised floor, or catch it with a CDU on the rack's back, a CRAC unit or an exhaust fan. LOOP: liquid-cooled modules (ASIC and up) send 85% of their heat down a Coolant Pipe, and CDUs on a rack's back, CRACs and reactors put heat into the same pipes. Every pipe network is one loop, and heat sinks on it take the heat out: Cooling Towers (wet with water from a Water Pump, dry without), Chillers and Water Heat Exchangers. A loop can only take what its sinks can; any extra stays in the air. Open a rack to see how much of its heat goes to the loop and how much to the air, and check a loop with the Multimeter.

Coolant Pipe

rackcraft:coolant_pipe

Each connected run of Coolant Pipe is one coolant loop. Heat goes in from racks with liquid-cooled modules, CDUs on a rack's back, CRAC units and Modular Reactors, and comes out at heat sinks: Cooling Towers, Chillers and Water Heat Exchangers. Run it as far as you like, so the heat can be dumped well away from the racks. Check a loop with the Multimeter: heat in versus what its sinks can take.

CRAC Unit

rackcraft:crac_unit

Computer-room air conditioner: takes heat out of the room air and puts it into its coolant loop. It pulls up to 40 kW from the air on each of its front and back faces, more the hotter that air is. Its loop needs sinks to take that heat. Draws 3 kW.

Cooling Tower

rackcraft:cooling_tower

Heat sink for a coolant loop. With a Water Pump on the same loop supplying it 4 units of water it runs wet and takes 120 kW out of the loop; it drinks that water, draining source blocks from the pump's shore. Without water it runs dry, blowing outside air across its radiator: 40 kW in temperate biomes, 60 kW in snowy ones and 24 kW in deserts, savannas and badlands. Part-fed, it makes up the difference. Draws 4 kW.

Water Pump

rackcraft:freshwater_pump

Supplies water to the Cooling Towers on its coolant loop. Place it touching water: a lake, a river or the sea all do. It draws 1.5 kW and supplies one unit per three water source blocks within six blocks, up to 16 units: four towers' worth. It drinks what it touches: every four minutes of one unit drains a source block from the shore. Only towers need water; Dry Coolers, Chillers and Water Heat Exchangers don't.

Chiller

rackcraft:chiller

Heavy-duty heat sink for a coolant loop: takes 250 kW out of the loop anywhere, indoors or out, with no water. It pays for it in power: 5 kW plus a fifth of the heat it moves (50 kW more at full tilt).

Water Heat Exchanger

rackcraft:water_heat_exchanger

Heat sink for a coolant loop that dumps heat into a body of water: place it touching water and it takes 3 kW per water source block within four blocks, up to 120 kW. Sea water works, and the water is never used up. Draws 0.5 kW.

Coolant Distribution Unit

rackcraft:cdu

Delivers liquid cooling directly to hardware. A Quantum Core only runs in a rack that has a CDU directly beside it. Draws 0.5 kW and sits on the coolant loop like a pipe.

Exhaust Fan

rackcraft:exhaust_fan

Pulls hot air out of the block in front of it and dumps it outside: up to 10 kW, only while that air is at least 1 C above ambient, so it idles (and stays clean) when there's nothing to move. Point it into a hot aisle. Moving heat outside makes smog in proportion to the heat moved; smog spreads to neighbouring chunks and dims solar panels, and past 30 it makes you dizzy, then come spells of blindness, a hacking cough, and from 70 poison. Wear a Respirator or run a Smog Scrubber. Draws 0.2 kW.

Raised Floor Tile

rackcraft:raised_floor_tile

A perforated floor panel that blocks airflow. Use it to seal the floor between hot and cold aisles so rack exhaust cannot creep back into the intakes.

Thermal Scanner

rackcraft:thermal_scanner

Hold it to see coloured heat particles in every warm air cell within 24 blocks: blue is cool and red is past 40 C. Right-click a rack while holding it to read the inlet and exhaust temperatures.

Smog Scrubber

rackcraft:smog_scrubber

Pulls smog back out of the air: at full power it removes 1.5 smog per second from its own chunk and 0.75 from each neighbouring chunk. It draws 6 kW, so it is cheaper than the poison. Place it near your exhaust fans.

Respirator

rackcraft:respirator

A half-face mask with charcoal filters, worn in the helmet slot. While you wear it smog can't touch you: no dizziness, blindness, cough or poison. The filter clogs in smog (about 40 minutes of it, faster when it's choking); repair it with charcoal on an anvil.
