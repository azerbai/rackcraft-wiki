Life in Orbit

Low Orbit is a place you can go: a Crew Capsule carries you up from the pad and a Return Capsule brings you home. It is vacuum: go up in a sealed Pressure Suit with Oxygen Canisters, wear Mag Boots to walk the deck, and build rooms of Pressure Hull with an Oxygen Vent to breathe without a suit. Racks up there mine 75% more RackCoin, but the only way to shed their heat is a coolant loop out to Radiator Panels, and only Liquid-Immersion Racks run outside a pressurised room. A Station Core keeps the station loaded while you're away, a Ground Link joins it to the network, Cargo Pods bring freight to a Docking Port, and Whipple Shields keep debris out of your rooms.

Station Truss

rackcraft:station_truss

The deck of a space station: aluminum truss under a steel walkway. You arrive in Low Orbit standing on a 5 x 5 of it, and it is what you build out from. Craft eight from six Aluminum Ingots and three Steel Ingots.

Crew Capsule

rackcraft:crew_capsule

A payload with a seat. Load it into a Launch Control like any other (it needs three stages), stand on the pad in a sealed Pressure Suit with at least a minute of air and press Launch: you ride the countdown and the climb strapped in on top. Above Y 600 you arrive in Low Orbit on your company's deck, with a Return Capsule beside you for the trip home. If the launch fails, the escape motor fires and you come down under a parachute. Built on the Assembly Line from a Return Capsule (eight Hydrogen Canisters for life support, four Electric Motors for the escape tower, a Respirator, weld, rivet) once Crewed Spaceflight is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Return Capsule: 5 steps needs Crewed Spaceflight

Return Capsule

rackcraft:return_capsule

Your ride home from Low Orbit. A Crew Capsule leaves one on your deck when you arrive. Use it in orbit and choose Deorbit: the heat shield takes the plasma of re-entry, the chute opens high up, and you drift down to where you launched from. It's spent on the way, so keep a spare. Built on the Assembly Line from a Satellite Bus (four Steel Blocks for the heat shield, two Cryo Coils, four Circuit Boards, weld, rivet) once Crewed Spaceflight is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Satellite Bus: 5 steps needs Crewed Spaceflight

Mag Boots

rackcraft:mag_boots

Boots with electromagnets in the soles. In Low Orbit there is no gravity: without them you float, rising when you jump and sinking when you sneak. Wear them and you walk normally on anything within three blocks under your feet; step off the edge of the deck and you float instead of falling. They are also the fourth piece of the Pressure Suit: its leggings seal onto them. Craft from Iron Boots, four Copper Wire, two Steel Ingots and a Circuit Board.

Oxygen Canister

rackcraft:oxygen_canister

Five minutes of air. An Electrolyser makes one beside every Hydrogen Canister while its by-product slot has room (with no room it just vents the oxygen and carries on). A Pressure Helmet pulls canisters from your inventory as it needs them, or use one to top it up. Oxygen Vents burn them to keep a room breathable.

Pressure Helmet

rackcraft:pressure_helmet

The bowl of a spacesuit, with a gold visor. It holds ten minutes of air (two Oxygen Canisters) and draws more from your inventory as it empties. Inside a pressurised room it fills back up from the room's air, empty to full in half a minute. It only works sealed to the rest of the suit: Pressure Suit, Pressure Leggings and Mag Boots. A Respirator is not a substitute: it filters air, and there is none in orbit to filter. Built on the Assembly Line from a Respirator (four Glass, four Aluminum Ingots, a Circuit Board, weld) once Crewed Spaceflight is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Respirator: 4 steps needs Crewed Spaceflight

rackcraft:pressure_hull

Airlock

rackcraft:airlock

A sliding hatch: airtight while shut, open to walk through. Use it to open or shut it, along with the Airlocks stacked above and below, so two make a door. It won't open if that would vent a pressurised room to space: build an airlock with two doors and shut one before opening the other, or sneak to force it and take the consequences. Craft two from four Pressure Hull, an Electric Motor and a Circuit Board.

Oxygen Vent

rackcraft:oxygen_vent

Breathable air for a space station room. It fills the space in front of its grille, up to 4,096 blocks, through everything except Pressure Hull, Pressure Glass and shut Airlocks. If that never reaches open space the room is sealed, and with power (2 kW) and Oxygen Canisters in its slot it is pressurised: no suit needed inside. It uses a canister every 10 minutes for each 512 blocks of room, and an Item Pipe keeps sixteen in it. Break the hull and the room vents, loudly. Does nothing on Earth, where there is plenty of air already.

Crafting
Iron bars

Whipple Shield

rackcraft:whipple_shield

Pressure Hull with a thin aluminum bumper standing off in front of it, so a piece of debris shatters on the bumper instead of going through. It seals a room like Pressure Hull, and shrugs off three Debris Field hits, denting a little more each time; the fourth goes through. Use an Aluminum Ingot on it to beat out a dent (mine a dented one and only the Pressure Hull comes back). Debris only ever hits a room's outer walls, so shield those first. Craft two from six Aluminum Ingots, two Steel Ingots and a Pressure Hull.

Radiator Panel

rackcraft:radiator_panel

The only way to get rid of heat in Low Orbit, where there's no air or water to dump it into. Put it on a coolant loop and it shines the loop's heat off into space from its two broad faces, front and back: 30 kW a face in sunlight and 45 kW in the Earth's shadow, so a panel with both faces clear takes 60 to 90 kW. A face only counts if the 8 blocks out from it are empty and outside any pressurised room, so build them out as wings. It needs no power. On Earth the air blankets it and it manages 5 kW a face. Craft one from four Aluminum Ingots, two Copper Ingots, two Coolant Pipe and a Steel Ingot.

Station Core

rackcraft:station_core

Keeps your station running while you're back on the ground. Racks only earn while their chunks are loaded, so a Station Core in Low Orbit holds the 5x5 of chunks round itself loaded (80 blocks each way), day and night, through restarts. Without power (4 kW) it still holds the 3x3 round itself, so put its solar near it. A company holds at most 49 chunks between all its cores; overlapping cores share. Break it and the chunks go back to sleep. Does nothing on Earth. Craft one from four Pressure Hull, two Circuit Boards, two Eyes of Ender and a Steel Block.

Ground Link

rackcraft:ground_link

A dish that ties a space station to the internet. Fiber networks don't reach orbit, so up there every router carries nothing until a linked Ground Link joins its fiber. Build one on Earth with power and open sky straight above it, and one on the station: the station dish carries 100 RC/s like an Uplink Router and switches on every router on its fiber. Without a link the station is off line in every way: no Ops Terminal, no Crypto Exchange, no Darknet, no Procurement Wall, and the Auto-Buyer can't shop. Each dish on the ground serves one on the station. A ground dish you're not near keeps working as it was when you left. Draws 6 kW at each end. Craft one from three Aluminum Ingots, two Circuit Boards, an Uplink Router, two Steel Ingots and a Fiber Cable.

Docking Port

rackcraft:docking_port

Where Cargo Pods arrive on your station. A pod launched from the ground docks at your company's first free Docking Port in Low Orbit (one with nothing in its slot), even with nobody up there, and the port files its cargo into the storage on its fiber, as much as fits, a little every second. The empty pod stays in the slot: take it out to free the port, and pack it again. A port in the Overworld takes pods sent home from a Launch Control on the Moon the same way. Needs no power. Craft one from four Pressure Hull, two Steel Ingots, a Circuit Board, an Electric Motor and a Chest.

Cargo Pod

rackcraft:cargo_pod

Freight for the station: a double chest that rides a rocket. Use it to pack it like a chest (54 slots; pods don't fit inside pods), then load it into a Launch Control's payload slot. It needs one Rocket Stage and a free Docking Port on your station, and docks there to be unloaded into the station's storage. If the launch fails, the pod's chute brings it and its cargo back to the pad. From a Launch Control on the Moon it goes the other way, home to a Docking Port on Earth: one stage and a Heat Shield. It keeps its load wherever it is, so the empty one can go round again. Craft one from four Aluminum Ingots, two Chests, two Steel Ingots and a Circuit Board.
