Wind and Solar at Scale

The Assembly Line tier of renewables. A Solar Array is six panels on one 3x2 frame (150 kW, and arrays that touch share power without cables). A Wind Tower is a nacelle on at least ten Tower Sections with a clear 5x5 in front for its blades: 600 kW at Y 130, up to 750 kW higher up: a farm of them can run a data hall. One wind blows for the whole world and rises and falls over a few minutes, so batteries smooth it out. These machines wear, losing up to 30% over three days of running, until a Maintenance Drone services them; the basic panel and turbine never wear. A Site Planner can build whole fields of them.

Solar Array Frame

rackcraft:solar_array_frame

The bare 3x2 frame of a Solar Array. On an Assembly Line it gets 6 Solar Panels, 2 Circuit Boards, a weld and rivets.

Crafting

Solar Array

rackcraft:solar_array

Six panels' worth of solar on one 3x2 frame, built on the Assembly Line. Place it like a bed: it takes three blocks to your right and two ahead, and needs them clear. In full sun it makes 150 kW (smog dims it, night stops it) and every array touching another shares its power, so a field of them needs no cables. It slowly wears down, losing up to 30% over three days of running, until a Maintenance Drone from a Drone Dock services it. Break any part and the whole array comes up as one item.

Tower Section

rackcraft:tower_section

One more block of Wind Tower, built on the Assembly Line from a Tower Frame (a weld and some rivets). Stack them straight up and put a Wind Tower Nacelle on top; power runs down the tower to the ground. Thin enough that air passes it.

Nacelle Frame

rackcraft:nacelle_frame

The empty shell of a Wind Tower Nacelle. On an Assembly Line it gets 8 Electric Motors (the generator), 4 Circuit Boards, a weld, 16 Copper Wire and rivets.

Crafting

Wind Tower Nacelle

rackcraft:wind_nacelle

The business end of a Wind Tower: a generator with three great blades, built on the Assembly Line. Put it on top of at least ten Tower Sections stacked in a column, facing into open air: the blades need a clear 5x5 in front of it. It makes 600 kW at Y 130, more higher up (up to 750 kW above Y 150), half again in a thunderstorm, and rises and falls with the wind like a turbine. A tall tower on low ground reaches the good wind anywhere. Its power comes down the tower to whatever touches the bottom section. It wears like a Solar Array, so keep a Drone Dock nearby.
