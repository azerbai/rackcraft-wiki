Utility Plants

Two more cube multiblocks (2x2x2 to 5x5x5), each doing something no other machine does. Their casings change when formed; they make no items, so they have no port.

PlantWhat it doesNumbers
Grid-Tie SubstationSells spare solar, wind and reactor output to the outside grid for RackCoin, once everything else is powered and batteries are charged. Never drains a battery, runs a diesel or resells the utility feed; exported reactor power still burns fuelUp to 1 MW per core; 0.05 RC per kJ at night, 60% of that before dusk, 2.5 times as much in the evening peak
Heat Recovery PlantA coolant-loop heat sink that sells the heat to nearby villages as district heating. Needs no power or water, only customers50 kW per villager within 64 blocks (half again in cold biomes, less in hot ones), up to 400 kW per core; 0.4 RC per kJ
