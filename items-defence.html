Arms and Ruins

Not everything out there is abandoned. Military Bases and the odd hostile data center have people in them, and they are not pleased to see you: on Peaceful they ignore you until you hit one, on Easy and Normal they act once you come close, on Hard they open fire on sight. The weapons here obey the mod's rules: batteries, hydrogen and heat. Directed Energy and Plasma Weapons in the R&D tab unlock them, and Mil-Spec Refits lets the Assembly Line rebuild each one to a Mk II. Every one of them sets off End Crystals; an EMP Grenade or a cut cable is a way in that needs no gun at all.

Hydrogen Flamethrower

rackcraft:hydrogen_flamethrower

Hold use to spray an 8-block jet of fire that droops a little like the burning fuel it is: it sets mobs alight, burns cobwebs and lights flammable plain blocks, never Rackcraft machines or chests. One Hydrogen Canister is about 20 seconds of fire. Heat builds slowly and it locks out for a few seconds at 100%. Needs Directed Energy. Once Mil-Spec Refits is researched, an Assembly Robot line rebuilds it to Mk II with two Hydrogen Tanks, four Cryo Coils, a gold Chiplet and eight Graphene Sheets: an oxygen-fed 18-block jet, faster and fatter, with three and a half times the damage, that splashes burning fuel round where it lands, sets the ground alight where its fuel comes down and burns twice as long per canister at half the heat.

Arc Coil

rackcraft:arc_coil

Click for lightning that hits a mob and chains to up to three more within 5 blocks. Zap a Rackcraft machine and every breaker on its power network trips; robots and drones take extra damage and freeze for 20 seconds. A Battery Cell is eight shots. Needs Directed Energy. Mk II (Mil-Spec Refits, on the Assembly Line with eight Superconducting Wire, a gold Chiplet and four Cryo Coils): half again the reach and damage, two more links in the chain, sixteen shots a cell and half the heat.

Railgun

rackcraft:railgun

Hold to charge for up to two seconds, release to fire a slug that pierces mobs and up to three plain blocks (never a Rackcraft block, never bedrock). Damage scales with charge, the recoil is not subtle. Each shot needs a Steel Slug and a quarter of a Battery Cell; it runs very hot and has a long cooldown. Needs Directed Energy. Mk II (Mil-Spec Refits, on the Assembly Line with twelve Superconducting Wire, four Steel Blocks and two Photonic Chips): 96 blocks of reach, half again the damage, two more blocks pierced, eight shots a cell and half the heat.

Plasma Rifle

rackcraft:plasma_rifle

Click for a bolt of plasma with splash damage and a glowing scorch. A Hydrogen Canister loads twelve bolts and a Battery Cell about thirty. It overheats in seconds unless a Coolant Pack is in your pack, in which case it keeps going and the pack slowly wears down. Needs Plasma Weapons. Mk II (Mil-Spec Refits, on the Assembly Line with a Photonic Tensor Core, four Cryo Coils and a Hydrogen Tank): half again the reach and damage, a wider splash, twice the bolts per canister and cell, and half the heat.

Crafting
Assembly Line

Lance Laser

rackcraft:lance_laser

Hold for a continuous beam up to 32 blocks that melts cobweb and ice (glass is safe, unless the config says otherwise), sets things alight and goes round armour. A Battery Cell is about 20 seconds of beam, and it heats up extremely fast: short bursts only. Needs Plasma Weapons. Mk II (Mil-Spec Refits, on the Assembly Line with six Photonic Chips, two gold Chiplets and four Graphene Sheets): a 48-block beam, half again the damage, twice as long per cell and half the heat.

EMP Grenade

rackcraft:emp_grenade

Throw it and every Rackcraft machine, drone, turret and security robot within 10 blocks switches off for three minutes. PDUs trip, robots freeze mid-step, vault doors give up and slide open. Nothing is damaged, only silenced. The polite way into a Military Base. Needs Directed Energy.

Coolant Pack

rackcraft:coolant_pack

A pack of cold stuff. Carry one and every energy weapon in your inventory vents ten times faster, which is the only way a Plasma Rifle keeps up. Each shot while the weapon is warm wears it down; when it is spent it is spent.

Laser Sentry

rackcraft:laser_sentry

A power-hungry turret on a swivel that shoots visible, dodgeable bolts at hostile mobs and angry guards within 16 blocks (never players, pets, villagers or drones). It draws 1.5 kW, checks for targets twice a second and heats up as it fires: put it on a coolant loop with a tower or chiller and it shoots all day, without one it overheats in seconds and sulks. Use it to cycle who it shoots. Needs Directed Energy.

Blast Door

rackcraft:blast_door

A vault door you cannot mine. It stays shut while a live Power Cable touches it and slides open the moment that cable is cut or broken, or an EMP goes off nearby. Nothing is destroyed; it just retracts, with a sulk. Found in the Military Base and in the odd hostile data center.
