Industry and Drones

Once RackCoin stops mattering, power is what's left to spend. The Electrolyser turns hundreds of megawatts into hydrogen: Hydrogen Canisters, or with a Hydrogen Tank on its network, gas piped straight into the tank (no ingots needed), which storage then hands out as canisters. An Exchange Auto-Buyer on a storage network buys whatever its machines run short of, and the Assembly Line turns parts into machines no crafting table can make: lay Conveyor Belts past a row of robot arms (Welding, Riveting and Assembly Robots, each facing the belt) and each arm does its step as a workpiece passes. A Drone Frame goes in one end and a Maintenance Drone comes out the other. Belt Loaders feed a line from storage and Belt Unloaders file what comes off it, and a Storage Exporter keeps any machine or chest beside it stocked from storage. Drones live in a Drone Dock and fix failed modules (always with the same kind), cut cables and tripped breakers by themselves, and service worn Solar Arrays and Wind Towers; on an Item Pipe the dock restocks itself from storage.

Electrolyser

rackcraft:electrolyser

Splits water into hydrogen with an absurd amount of electricity: each core rolls an Aluminum Ingot into a tank and fills it, one Hydrogen Canister every 30 seconds, drawing 4,000 kW while it works. With a Hydrogen Tank touching it (or on the same Item Pipe or Storage Link network) it fills the tank instead, with no ingots at all, and stops only when the tanks are full. A multiblock: build a solid cube of 2x2x2 to 5x5x5 (or 10x10x10 with research) with water against its outside (any water, sea water too; it is never used up). A 3x3x3 pulls close to 100 MW and a 5x5x5 about 400 MW, so this is where a finished facility's spare power goes. Bigger cubes use up to 20% less power per core. Hydrogen fuels Maintenance Drones, and it is the rocket fuel for the launch programme. Feed and empty it like the other processing cubes; canisters gather in the port.

Hydrogen Canister

rackcraft:hydrogen_canister

An aluminum tank of compressed hydrogen from an Electrolyser: about 120 MJ of electricity went into it. A Drone Dock burns one every eight trips, an Assembly Robot can fit one into a drone as its fuel tank, and a launch pad will want them by the thousand.

Hydrogen Tank

rackcraft:hydrogen_tank

Holds hydrogen as gas, once Cryogenic Hydrogen Storage is researched. Each block holds 256 canisters' worth, and a solid cube (2x2x2 up to 5x5x5, or 10x10x10 with research) holds more per block: 40% more in a 5x5x5. An Electrolyser touching a tank, or on the same Item Pipe or Storage Link network, pipes its hydrogen straight in and needs no Aluminum Ingots. Storage counts the tank's hydrogen as Hydrogen Canisters, so Drone Docks, Site Planners, Launch Controls and anything else that takes canisters from storage draw on the tank. Its slot holds Tanker Drones, which carry hydrogen to docks, planners and Launch Controls up to 256 blocks away. Needs no power.

Tanker Drone

rackcraft:tanker_drone

A big, fast hydrogen hauler, and the one drone you can't build: it is only sold at the Crypto Exchange, for a great deal of RackCoin, and only once Cryogenic Hydrogen Storage is researched. Put it in a Hydrogen Tank's slot and it flies a stack of canisters at a time from the tank to any Drone Dock, Site Planner or Launch Control within 256 blocks whose hydrogen slot isn't full, then comes back for more. Docks on the tank's own pipes already draw from it, so tankers serve the far ones.

Conveyor Belt

rackcraft:conveyor_belt

Carries one item at a time along the way it faces, a block a second. Drop an item on it, right-click it with one, or feed it with a hopper; right-click with an empty hand to take the item back. At the far end the item moves onto the next belt (which can turn), into a chest, hopper or any container, or drops off the end. A Robot Arm facing a belt stops whatever it can work on in the middle of that belt, does its step, and lets it go. Lay belts past a row of arms and you have an Assembly Line. Players and mobs standing on a running belt get carried too.

Belt Loader

rackcraft:belt_loader

Feeds an Assembly Line straight from storage. Put it on an Item Pipe that reaches a Storage Array, face it at the first Conveyor Belt, and put one of the item you want in its slot (that one is only a sample and stays put). Whenever the belt in front is empty it takes another from storage and puts it on, up to one a second. Needs no power.

Belt Unloader

rackcraft:belt_unloader

Files whatever comes off the end of an Assembly Line into storage. Point the last Conveyor Belt into it and put it on an Item Pipe that reaches a Storage Array: finished drones, arrays, nacelles and the rest land in its nine-slot buffer and go into storage once a second. Hoppers can empty it too. Needs no power.

Storage Exporter

rackcraft:storage_exporter

Sends items from storage into whatever it faces. Put it on an Item Pipe that reaches a Storage Array, face it at a machine or container (a Site Planner, a Launch Control, a chest), and put samples of what to send in its nine slots: once a second it tops the block in front up to a stack of each, through the face it touches, so a machine only gets what its slots take. The samples stay put. Needs no power.

Welding Robot

rackcraft:welding_arm

A robot arm with a plasma torch. Place it beside a Conveyor Belt, facing the belt, and it welds every workpiece that needs welding as it passes: six seconds per weld, drawing 1,500 kW while the torch is lit and 1 kW waiting. Below full power it welds slower, and under 10% it stops. Sparks fly; don't stand in them.

Riveting Robot

rackcraft:riveting_arm

A robot arm with a pneumatic rivet gun. Place it beside a Conveyor Belt, facing the belt, and it rivets every workpiece that needs riveting as it passes: four seconds a job, drawing 600 kW while it hammers and 1 kW waiting. Loud.

Assembly Robot

rackcraft:assembly_arm

A robot arm with a gripper and nine slots of parts. Place it beside a Conveyor Belt, facing the belt, and stock it with what the line needs (Electric Motors, Circuit Boards, Hydrogen Canisters): it installs parts into every workpiece whose next step it can do, three seconds per step at 250 kW, and keeps going while the steps that follow are installs too. Hoppers and Item Pipes can restock it, and on an Item Pipe it fetches for itself: it watches the belt in front of it and the five before it, and takes whatever part the next workpiece needs from storage (or buys it, if an Auto-Buyer is on the network).

Drone Frame

rackcraft:drone_frame

The bare airframe of a Maintenance Drone. Put it on an Assembly Line: an Assembly Robot fits four Electric Motors, a Welding Robot welds the mounts, Assembly Robots fit two Circuit Boards and a Hydrogen Canister, and a Riveting Robot closes the shell. A frame that falls off the line half-built remembers how far it got; its tooltip says what it needs next.

Maintenance Drone

rackcraft:maintenance_drone

A quadcopter with a gripper and a strong sense of duty. Load it into a Drone Dock and it flies out to fix things on its own: failed rack modules, cut cables and tripped breakers. It is built on an Assembly Line, not a crafting table.

Drone Dock

rackcraft:drone_dock

Home base for Maintenance Drones, and the end of walking to every broken thing in a big hall. Load drones into its first slot (up to eight), Hydrogen Canisters into the second, and spares into the rest: rack modules and Repair Kits. Better, put it on an Item Pipe that reaches storage: it then takes drones, hydrogen, Repair Kits and replacement modules from storage as it needs them, always the same kind of module that failed, and files the dead ones away. Every couple of seconds it looks for work within 32 blocks across (and 96 up or down, so a Wind Tower's nacelle is in reach) and sends a drone to each job: swapping a Failed Module out of a rack for a spare (the dead one comes back to the dock), splicing a cut cable with a Repair Kit, resetting a tripped PDU breaker, or servicing a worn Solar Array or Wind Tower. Each trip burns an eighth of a canister. Draws 2 kW. Hit a drone and it drops as an item, so maybe don't.
