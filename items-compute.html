Compute

A rack has eight bays, one module each. Earnings need power of at least 50% satisfaction, a fiber link to a router, and a cool intake. Above 27 C performance falls, and at 40 C the rack stops. A rack whose power drops below 50% trips and stays off until its inlet is under 32 C. The biggest module of all, the Wafer-Scale Engine, comes from a Wafer Fab once you've researched it: see R&D and the Frontier. Bigger racks, with 12 to 24 bays, come later: see Advanced Racks and Modules.

Server Rack

rackcraft:server_rack

The heart of the facility, with eight module bays: any module fits any bay, one per bay, so a rack takes eight GPU Blades as happily as eight Pi Nodes. Air comes in the front and every kilowatt it draws comes out as heat: out of the back as hot air, or, for liquid-cooled modules, 85% of it down its coolant pipe. A rack mines RackCoin (RC) when powered, networked by fiber to a router, and kept cool. Its screen shows the live mining rate or exactly what is stopping it, and where its heat is going. Use the 25/50/75/100% buttons to cap its load.

ASIC Miner

rackcraft:asic_miner

A module built only to mine crypto. It draws 0.4 to 1.2 kW and mines 5 RC per second at full load, cheap for what it earns, but it runs hot. Like all tier 3 hardware it is water-cooled: it needs a Water Pump on its rack's coolant network (1 unit).

GPU Blade

rackcraft:gpu_blade

An accelerator card. It draws 1 to 3 kW and mines 12 RC per second at full load. Plan for its heat. Water-cooled: it needs 2 units from a Water Pump. It also lends 10 AI compute to contract work, which pays a little better than mining.

Crafting Coprocessor

rackcraft:crafting_coprocessor

A rack module built for autocrafting: 8 general compute (four 1U Servers' worth) for 0.9 kW, and air-cooled, so it needs no coolant loop. It can't mine, and lends only 0.5 AI compute. Racks full of them turn a Storage Terminal's job queue into a production line. Boots in 4 seconds.
