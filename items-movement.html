Getting Around

A campus is a long walk. A Grapple gets you onto a roof, a Hydrogen Jetpack gets you over it and lets you hover, a Mag-Lev line runs a car between stations, and Quantum Teleport Pads skip the trip entirely for power and a little hydrogen. Personal Propulsion, Magnetic Levitation and Quantum Entanglement in the R&D tab unlock them.

Grapple

rackcraft:grapple_hook

Use it to fire a hook at the block you are looking at, up to 40 blocks away, and be pulled to it, faster as you go. Sneak to let go. It burns a Battery Cell a shot, and the last bit of the fall at the end is cushioned.

Hydrogen Jetpack

rackcraft:hydrogen_jetpack

A chestplate with a thrust bottle. Hold jump to climb, sneak in the air to hover (half the fuel), sprint while climbing to boost. It burns a Hydrogen Canister for every thirty seconds of thrust, taken from the canisters you carry or a Wireless Terminal's storage. Run dry in a long fall and it vents the last of its gas once, every five minutes, to slow you down. Mind the people below: the exhaust sets alight anything standing under a thrusting pack. Inert until Personal Propulsion is researched.

Mag-Lev Rail

rackcraft:maglev_rail

A slim rail for a Mag-Lev line; three to a craft. Rails and Stations that touch form a line a car can run along, and a rail is also a power-network block, so it carries power from any cable it touches. A cut rail breaks the line: a Repair Kit splices it. Inert until Magnetic Levitation is researched.

Mag-Lev Station

rackcraft:maglev_station

Where a line starts and ends. Right-click to ride to its destination (the nearest station until you choose); sneak-right-click with an empty hand to cycle destinations down the line. The car carries one rider, glides at up to 54 blocks a second, and draws 800 kW from this station's network while it runs: under 10% of that and it stalls, with an apology. Break a rail ahead of it and it stops there. Draws 0.5 kW otherwise.

Quantum Teleport Pad

rackcraft:teleport_pad

Stand on it for two seconds and you arrive on the pad it is entangled with, anywhere in the dimension. Entangle two pads by using a Linked Shard on one and then the other (a Dimensional Shard for pads in different dimensions, at twenty times the cost); sneak-use a shard to cut a pair. Each pad needs a Quantum Annealer in its slot and a Cryostat touching it. A jump burns a Hydrogen Canister from the source's Cryostat and a burst of power from its network, a megawatt-second per 100 blocks, so the grid has to be able to answer: the screen shows what a jump costs. The pad you land on only needs its Annealer and a stocked Cryostat. Needs room above both pads. Inert until Quantum Entanglement is researched.

Linked Shard

rackcraft:linked_shard

Half of an entangled pair, waiting for a pad. Use it on a Quantum Teleport Pad, then on a second one, and the two are entangled and the shard is used up. Sneak-use it on a pad to cut its pair.

Dimensional Shard

rackcraft:dimensional_shard

A Linked Shard with a Nether Star in it. It entangles pads in different dimensions, which cost twenty times the power and four times the hydrogen to jump between.
