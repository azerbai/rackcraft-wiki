What Gemerald Cloud Built

Spoilers. The Field Manual keeps this chapter for late in the game.

Gemerald Cloud's weights were sold off at the liquidation, cheap, to a new company in a dirt hut. That was you: every Gemerald model you trained started as theirs, and HEROBRINE-1 is called 1 because there was a 0. HEROBRINE-0 is still walled in under the Military Base, which is why its generator must never stop and why cutting its cable opens the gate. The backup went to the Moon with Nebula Compute, on a tape marked DO NOT RUN, and their lunar data center crashed. Nobody has run it. Probably nobody ever will. The museum is yours now: the IPO Plaque would look good in the next case along.
