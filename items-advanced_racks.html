Advanced Racks and Modules

Three bigger racks, each built on the Assembly Line from the one below and each behind its own research. The High-Density Rack has 12 bays and a built-in radiator door. The Liquid-Immersion Rack has 16 bays and puts all its heat into the coolant loop. The Exascale Cabinet has 24 bays, runs everything 20% faster on half the fiber bandwidth, and only takes tier 2 hardware and up. New modules all start as a Blade Chassis; the first part fitted decides what it becomes. Quantum Annealers mine twice what a Quantum Core does but need a Cryostat beside their rack, fed with hydrogen. NPUs serve contracts and leases only, and FPGAs can be reflashed between jobs.

High-Density Rack

rackcraft:high_density_rack

Tier 2: twelve module bays and a radiator door built into its back. On a coolant loop, the door catches up to 40 kW of the rack's hot air into the loop before it reaches the room, on top of the 85% that liquid-cooled modules send down the pipe; off a loop it is just a bigger rack. Its fans draw 0.5 kW. It can't be crafted: an Assembly Robot fits a Server Rack with two Cryo Coils, a CDU and four Coolant Pipes, then it is welded and riveted, once High-Density Racks is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Server Rack: 5 steps needs High-Density Racks

Liquid-Immersion Rack

rackcraft:immersion_rack

Tier 3: sixteen bays, with every module sunk in dielectric fluid. All of its heat goes into the coolant loop and none into the air, so a hall of them needs no CRACs or fans at all, only sinks. It won't run off a loop, even with air-cooled modules in it, and its pumps draw 2 kW. It's also the space rack: sealed in its bath it needs no air, so in Low Orbit it runs out in the open on a loop of Radiator Panels, where every other rack needs a pressurised room. Built on the Assembly Line from a High-Density Rack, two CDUs, eight Coolant Pipe and four Graphene Sheets, once Immersion Cooling is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from High-Density Rack: 5 steps needs Immersion Cooling

Exascale Cabinet

rackcraft:exascale_cabinet

Tier 4: twenty-four bays on a photonic fabric. Everything in it mines and lends compute 20% faster, its mining needs only half the fiber bandwidth, and like an immersion rack all its heat goes to the loop. It only takes tier 2 hardware and up (no Pi Nodes, 1U Servers, ASIC Miners or Crafting Coprocessors), and its switches draw 30 kW whenever it has anything in it. Built on the Assembly Line from a Liquid-Immersion Rack with eight Photonic Interconnects, four Superconducting Wire, a Core Router and four HBM Stacks, once Exascale Architecture is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Liquid-Immersion Rack: 6 steps needs Exascale Architecture

Blade Chassis

rackcraft:blade_chassis

An empty module sled: the base for every new module made on the Assembly Line. The first part an Assembly Robot fits decides what it becomes: GPU Chips make an FPGA Module, bronze Chiplets an NPU, silver Chiplets a Neuromorphic Core, gold Chiplets a Photonic Tensor Core, and Superconducting Wire a Quantum Annealer.

Crafting

FPGA Module

rackcraft:fpga_module

Reprogrammable hardware: right-click it to reflash it for mining (8 RC/s), AI (8 AI compute) or autocrafting (20 general compute), about two thirds of what the specialist module does in each. Draws 0.4 to 2.5 kW and is air-cooled. Take it out of the rack to reflash it. Built on the Assembly Line from a Blade Chassis (two GPU Chips, two Graphene Sheets, two RAM Modules, weld, rivet) once Advanced Materials is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Blade Chassis: 5 steps needs Advanced Materials

NPU Inference Card

rackcraft:npu_card

An inference accelerator: 32 AI compute on 0.3 to 1.8 kW, air-cooled, but only for contract generation and Compute Leases. It never helps train models, R&D or frontier runs. It can't mine. Built on the Assembly Line from a Blade Chassis (four bronze Chiplets, an HBM Stack, two Circuit Boards, rivet) once Chiplets and Advanced Packaging is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Blade Chassis: 4 steps needs Chiplets and Advanced Packaging

Neuromorphic Core

rackcraft:neuromorphic_core

Spiking silicon modelled on a brain: 80 general compute for autocrafting and general research on just 0.2 to 1.2 kW, air-cooled. It can't mine and lends little AI. Built on the Assembly Line from a Blade Chassis (two silver Chiplets, an HBM Stack, two Graphene Sheets, weld, rivet) once Chiplets and Advanced Packaging is researched.

Assembly Line
Starts from Blade Chassis: 5 steps needs Chiplets and Advanced Packaging

Photonic Tensor Core

rackcraft:photonic_tensor_core

Matrix maths done in light: 300 AI compute and 60 general compute in one bay, twice a Wafer-Scale Engine, on 3 to 12 kW. Liquid-cooled; it can't mine. Built on the Assembly Line from a Blade Chassis (two gold Chiplets, four Photonic Interconnects, two HBM Stacks, weld, rivet) once Silicon Photonics is researched. Hardware failures never take one out.

Assembly Line
Starts from Blade Chassis: 5 steps needs Silicon Photonics

Quantum Annealer

rackcraft:quantum_annealer

The best miner there is: 120 RC/s, twice a Quantum Core, on 4 to 14 kW, liquid-cooled. Its rack only runs with a cold Cryostat directly beside it, and each running Annealer boils off a Hydrogen Canister every 5 minutes. It boots in 25 seconds. Built on the Assembly Line from a Blade Chassis (four Superconducting Wire, a gold Chiplet, four Cryo Coils, an HBM Stack, weld, rivet) once Quantum Annealing is researched. Hardware failures never take one out.

Assembly Line
Starts from Blade Chassis: 6 steps needs Quantum Annealing

Topological Core

rackcraft:topological_core

The best general-purpose module there is: 100 RC/s of mining, 120 general compute (more than a Neuromorphic Core) and 120 AI compute in one bay, on 5 to 20 kW, liquid-cooled. Its qubits are braided anyons, so they hardly decohere, but like a Quantum Core its rack only runs with a CDU directly beside it. It boots in 30 seconds. Built on the Assembly Line from a Blade Chassis (two Quantum Cores, two gold Chiplets, four Superconducting Wire, two HBM Stacks and two Photonic Interconnects, weld, rivet) once Topological Qubits is researched. Hardware failures never take one out.

Assembly Line
Starts from Blade Chassis: 8 steps needs Topological Qubits

Cryostat

rackcraft:cryostat

Keeps Quantum Annealers a whisker above absolute zero. A rack holding Annealers only runs with a cold Cryostat directly beside it: one with power (15 kW) and Hydrogen Canisters in its slot. The liquid hydrogen boils off as it works, one canister every 5 minutes for each running Annealer beside it (a rack between two Cryostats splits the load). On an Item Pipe it keeps sixteen canisters from storage or a Hydrogen Tank. Can't be bought.

Crafting

Pod Port

rackcraft:pod_port

Turns a solid block of High-Density Racks or better into a Compute Pod. Build a solid cuboid of at least four racks (no more than 2 x 4 x 8, and 64 in all), touch it with a powered Pod Port, and put Photonic Interconnects in its slot, one for every four racks: the fabric then lends 10% more AI compute from 4 racks, 20% from 8 and 30% from 16. The cost is one breaker: if any rack in the pod overheats, the whole pod trips and lends nothing until every rack has cooled. Its screen fills and empties every bay in the pod at once. Draws 0.5 kW. Inert until Compute Pods is researched.
