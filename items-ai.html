AI Contracts

Racks can do more than mine. Clients post contracts at the Operations Terminal: an image of something absurd, or an essay, a legal document, somebody's homework. Accept one, press Generate and pick a cluster (it needs an uplink router), and its racks work until the item reaches the required quality; finished work lands in the outbox and is delivered automatically. Pay beats mining on the same racks, more so with Tensor Accelerators, and late work earns half. Each kind of work has a lineup of models, like a real lab's: Lite models are fast and learn from a few examples but top out early, Pro models are slow and data-hungry but end up far better, and Flash sits between. Pick one per contract, or leave it on Auto for the fastest that reaches the quality. Every model trains separately, on training data: children drawing at a Kids' Art Table, and librarians shackled to Scriptorium Desks. Upload what they make to a model at the terminal and idle racks train it. Keep spare finished work: clients often order the same thing again.

Operations Terminal

rackcraft:operations_terminal

Runs the whole facility from one screen: the AI contract board (accept, pick a model, generate, deliver), what every cluster of racks is doing and its policy, the model lineup and each model's training, RackCoin income, and every problem with racks, storage, pumps, cables, generators and smog in this dimension. It needs no power or cables. Players within 64 blocks of one are told when an event starts.

Kids' Art Table

rackcraft:art_table

Training data for the image model. Stock it with paper and Crayons and baby villagers come running to draw. Each kid finishes a drawing every 20 seconds (up to four kids); four drawings make a Crayon Art Aggregate. Kids at a stocked table never seem to grow up. Connect it to a Storage Array with Item Pipe and it restocks itself and sends the aggregates back to storage; hoppers work too.

Crayon Art Aggregate

rackcraft:art_aggregate

Four children's drawings, scanned and tagged. Upload it to one of the image models (SketchDiffusion, Nano Melon, Nano Melon Pro) at an Operations Terminal.

Scriptorium Desk

rackcraft:writing_desk

Training data for the language model. Use Shackles on a librarian within six blocks to chain them to the desk, then stock it with paper and ink sacs. The librarian writes a Librarian Text Corpus every 30 seconds from two paper; an ink sac lasts four. Connect it to a Storage Array with Item Pipe and it restocks itself and files the corpora in storage. They can't leave and complain about it constantly. Sneak-right-click them with an empty hand to set them free.

Shackles

rackcraft:shackles

Use on a librarian near a free Scriptorium Desk to chain them to it. You can see the chain from their wrists to the desk. Only librarians can write.

Librarian Text Corpus

rackcraft:text_corpus

Several thousand words a librarian really didn't want to write. Upload it to one of the Gemerald language models (Flash-Lite, Flash, Pro) at an Operations Terminal.

Generated Image

rackcraft:generated_image

An AI-generated image, made for a contract. It remembers its prompt and quality: keep spares, because clients often ask for the same thing again.
