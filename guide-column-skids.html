Column Skids

The hands-on way to grow a data hall: cheaper than a Site Planner quote, because nothing is bought at the Exchange and no drone flies, but you make the parts, pack them and hook the row up yourself.

  • Column Skid Frame: a steel pallet (two Steel Ingots either side of a Steel Block, two more on top, three Aluminum Ingots under it).
  • Packing: put the frame on an Assembly Line. The racks an Assembly Robot fits first decide the skid: four Server Racks, High-Density Racks, Immersion Racks or Exascale Cabinets. Then come two CDUs, a weld, the Chillers and Core Routers, and rivets. Each skid gets as many Chillers and Core Routers as a Data Hall column of that rack tier full of its default module (Quantum Cores where the tier takes them): two Chillers and two Core Routers for Server Racks, more for the denser tiers. The tooltip gives the counts. The High-Density, Immersion and Exascale skids wait for Dense Racks, Immersion Cooling and Exascale.
  • Unpacking: use a skid on the ground and the column unfolds away from you, one block wide and three deep: the near racks face you, a CDU behind them, and the far racks face away, so both racks' backs sit on the CDU. They are two high, with Core Routers over the racks and Chillers over the CDU. It needs the whole space clear (it says where something is in the way) and keeps the skid if it can't go.
  • A row of skids is a hall row: set side by side, every block touches, so power, coolant and fiber need no cables between them. Run one power cable to the row, and leave an aisle in front and behind.
  • Filling: skids come with empty racks. As in a Data Hall, a row's near racks and far racks are two clusters (each side has its own Core Routers; the CDUs and Chillers between them carry no fiber), so press Fill bays on both in the Operations Terminal and every bay is filled from storage.
