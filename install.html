Install and first steps

Rackcraft 2.8.0 runs on Minecraft 1.20.1 with Fabric.

What you need

  • Minecraft 1.20.1 and Fabric Loader 0.14.21 or newer
  • Fabric API
  • GeckoLib 4.8.4 or newer
  • Java 17

Put the Rackcraft jar in your mods folder alongside them. Everything else is optional: JEI, Jade, Create, FTB Teams and FTB Quests, TaCZ and Superb Warfare all work with it, and without them Rackcraft plays the same. See Other Mods.

Two add-ons are separate jars: Quartermaster (the arms counter and vehicle dealership) and the Grid Charger (charges Superb Warfare vehicles from your grid, and repairs and restocks ones parked close by).

Your first hour

Power goes to racks, racks mine RackCoin, and RackCoin buys everything else at the Crypto Exchange.

  1. Read the Field Manual, which you can craft. Its first chapter covers the start.
  2. Build a generator, a Server Rack and an Uplink Router, then wire them with Power Cable and Fiber.
  3. Fill the rack with modules and watch where the heat goes: Cooling is where most first facilities go wrong.
  4. Sell at the Crypto Exchange, then follow Progression.
