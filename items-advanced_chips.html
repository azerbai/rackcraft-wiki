Advanced Materials and Chips

Past the Wafer-Scale Engine, hardware is manufactured, not crafted or bought. Four research projects open it up. Advanced Materials (after Cryogenic Hydrogen Storage) unlocks two new processing cubes, the CVD Furnace for Graphene and the Epitaxy Reactor for Gallium Nitride, both hungry for hydrogen, and Superconducting Wire on the Assembly Line. Chiplets and Advanced Packaging (after Extreme UV Lithography) lets an Assembly Line dice a Wafer-Scale Engine into eight chiplets. That is the silicon lottery: the whole wafer bins as one, 60% bronze, 30% silver and only 10% gold, and better modules need better bins. Silicon Photonics turns Gallium Nitride and silver chiplets into Photonic Interconnects. None of these materials or chips is sold on the Crypto Exchange.

CVD Furnace

rackcraft:cvd_furnace

Grows Graphene Sheets by chemical vapour deposition: four Coke and a Hydrogen Canister make two sheets, a minute per batch per core, 150 kW per working core. A multiblock like the other processing cubes (2x2x2 and up), fed and emptied by hand, hopper or Item Pipe; on a pipe to a Hydrogen Tank it draws its hydrogen from the tank. It does nothing until Advanced Materials is researched.

Graphene Sheet

rackcraft:graphene_sheet

Carbon one atom thick: stronger than steel, better at heat than copper. Only a CVD Furnace makes it, from Coke and hydrogen. Goes into FPGAs, HBM Stacks, Neuromorphic Cores, wafer dicing and Liquid-Immersion Racks. Can't be bought.

Epitaxy Reactor

rackcraft:epitaxy_reactor

Recovers the trace of gallium in bauxite and grows it into crystal: eight Raw Bauxite and a Hydrogen Canister make one Gallium Nitride, 90 seconds per batch per core, 250 kW per working core. A processing cube (2x2x2 and up), locked until Advanced Materials is researched.

Gallium Nitride

rackcraft:gallium_nitride

A blue-violet crystal grown in an Epitaxy Reactor from bauxite's gallium. The base of a Photonic Interconnect. Can't be bought.

Superconducting Wire

rackcraft:superconducting_wire

Wire with no resistance at all, as long as it stays very cold. Built on the Assembly Line once Advanced Materials is researched: a Cryo Coil wound with eight Copper Wire, two Hydrogen Canisters of coolant, then welded and riveted. Used in Cryostats, Quantum Annealers and Exascale Cabinets.

Chiplet (Bronze Bin)

rackcraft:chiplet_bronze

A die cut from a wafer that tested slow. Once Chiplets and Advanced Packaging is researched, an Assembly Line dices a Wafer-Scale Engine (weld, two Graphene Sheets, rivet) into eight chiplets, and the whole wafer bins as one: 60% bronze, 30% silver, 10% gold. Bronze goes into HBM Stacks and NPU Inference Cards. A higher bin can always be crafted down to a lower one.

Chiplet (Silver Bin)

rackcraft:chiplet_silver

A die that tested well: 30% of diced wafers bin silver. Neuromorphic Cores and Photonic Interconnects need silver. Craft one into a bronze chiplet if you need bronze more.

Chiplet (Gold Bin)

rackcraft:chiplet_gold

The silicon lottery's jackpot: only one wafer in ten bins gold. The Photonic Tensor Core needs two and the Quantum Annealer one. Craft it into a silver chiplet if you must, but you'll regret it.

HBM Memory Stack

rackcraft:hbm_stack

Four memory dies stacked on a chiplet and bonded with graphene. Built on the Assembly Line from a RAM Module: three more RAM Modules, a bronze Chiplet and a Graphene Sheet, then riveted. Every tier 3 and 4 module and the Exascale Cabinet use them.

Assembly Line
Starts from RAM Module: 4 steps needs Chiplets and Advanced Packaging

Photonic Interconnect

rackcraft:photonic_chip

Moves data as light. Built on the Assembly Line from Gallium Nitride once Silicon Photonics is researched: a silver Chiplet, four Fiber Cable, welded and riveted. Four go in a Photonic Tensor Core and eight in an Exascale Cabinet.

Assembly Line
Starts from Gallium Nitride: 4 steps needs Silicon Photonics
