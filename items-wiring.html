Building and Wiring

Past the first hall, the work is routing. A Trunk Bundle carries power, coolant and fiber as one; the Cable Planner draws a run before you lay it; a Patch Panel rewires from a face click; Pylons and Power Beacons carry power where cable cannot go. The Constructor's Gauntlet and Terraformer Cannon do the heavy lifting by hand. Structured Cabling, Long Reach and Wireless Power in the R&D tab unlock the machines.

Blueprint

rackcraft:blueprint

A sheet of the future. Blank until a Blueprint Scanner writes a box of Rackcraft blocks into it: their kinds, which way they face, and for a rack the module that fills most of its bays. Put a written one in a Site Planner's material slots and pick the Blueprint layout, and the drones print it again wherever the site is.

Blueprint Scanner

rackcraft:blueprint_scanner

Use on a block for one corner and another for the opposite; the box is outlined while you hold it. Use in the air to scan it into a blank Blueprint in your inventory, up to 64 blocks a side. It only reads Rackcraft blocks (the printer's drones carry nothing else), and nothing inside machines but a rack's main module. Needs Digital Twin researched.

Trunk Bundle

rackcraft:trunk_bundle

One thick conduit that carries power, coolant and fiber at the same time: three networks in one block, instead of three runs side by side. It joins any power cable, coolant pipe or fiber cable it touches, and machines of those kinds, each on its own lane. A cut trunk cuts all three lanes at once, and one splice from a Repair Kit mends them. Inert until Structured Cabling is researched.

Cable Planner

rackcraft:cable_planner

Draw a cable run before you lay it. Right-click a block face to set the start, another for the end: a ghost of the route hangs in the world, red where something is in the way. Right-click in the air to cycle the route style (Straight, Zig-zag, Shortest, Hug the Walls) and sneak-right-click in the air to cycle the cable kind, Use a block face a third time to lay the whole route, taking the cables from your inventory (or from a Wireless Terminal's storage). Sneak-use on a block to clear the route.

Patch Panel

rackcraft:patch_panel

Rewire without touching a cable. Each of its six faces has a port number from 0 to 8: cables and machines on two faces with the same port (not 0) are joined, on every network at once; a face on port 0 is isolated. Right-click a face with an empty hand to cycle its port, use a Name Tag to label the panel, and read the wiring with a Multimeter. It carries nothing itself, so a panel with every face on port 0 is a very expensive wall. Inert until Structured Cabling is researched.

Pylon

rackcraft:pylon

A lattice mast for long-span power. Link two Pylons up to 128 blocks apart with a Pylon Linker and they behave as one cable, strung with an overhead line that sags across the gap. Every link wastes 3% of the network's load per 64 blocks of span. Both ends must be loaded to carry power, and a mast with an unloaded partner just carries nothing. Needs Structured Cabling.

Superconducting Pylon

rackcraft:superconducting_pylon

A Pylon wound with Superconducting Wire. Two of these link up to 256 blocks apart and waste nothing at all. A link to an ordinary Pylon falls back to the ordinary range and loss.

Pylon Linker

rackcraft:pylon_linker

Use it on a Pylon, then on another, to string a span between them. Sneak-use on a linked Pylon to cut its spans. Range 128 blocks, or 256 between two Superconducting Pylons.

Power Beacon

rackcraft:power_beacon

Beams power through the air to any Beacon Receiver within 16 blocks, no cable needed. It draws what the receivers' network asks for, up to 1 MW, plus 15% lost to the sky. Receivers are shared out evenly, and a beam is a line of sparks you can follow. Needs Wireless Power.

Beacon Receiver

rackcraft:beacon_receiver

The catching end of a Power Beacon: it pairs with the nearest beacon in range and feeds whatever power network it touches, drawn before any other source. Out of range, it just sits there looking smug.

Constructor's Gauntlet

rackcraft:constructor_gauntlet

A powered glove that builds from a distance. It reaches 32 blocks, takes blocks from the storage network of a Wireless Terminal in your inventory and returns what it deletes there, and spends a Battery Cell for every 16 uses. Sneak-use in the air to cycle modes: Line, Plane, Box fill, Swap and Delete. Right-click a block to start a selection, again to finish it. Swap replaces every block in the box with the block in your off hand. Long Reach research stretches it to 128 blocks.

Crafting

Terraformer Cannon

rackcraft:terraformer_cannon

Flattens, fills or hollows ground in a radius, working outward from where it hits over a moment. Right-click a block to fire at it. Sneak-use in the air to cycle the setting (Flatten, Fill, Hollow, Smooth; round or square); use it in the air to cycle the radius from 3 to 16. Every 256 blocks moved burns a Hydrogen Canister. It never touches a Rackcraft machine, a chest or bedrock.

Crafting

Heavy Terraformer

rackcraft:heavy_terraformer

The Terraformer Cannon scaled up for airfields and pads: radius 4 to 64 in steps of 4, round or square, and four times the ground per Hydrogen Canister (1,024 blocks). Right-click a block to fire; use in the air for the radius, sneak-use in the air for the setting (Flatten, Fill, Hollow, Smooth; round or square). A shot works outward over a few seconds rather than all at once. It never touches a Rackcraft machine, a chest or bedrock.
