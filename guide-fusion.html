Fusion

A Tokamak is a real ring, not a cube: a closed circle of plasma channel (one block of air) wrapped in a solid 3 x 3 shell, with a column through the hole in the middle. Research Magnetic Confinement first (after Lunar Landing; 400,000,000 RC + 4,000M general).

The ring's radius can be 6 to 24 and it has to be round: the channel may stray at most a block and a half from a true circle, and it must be one closed loop, one block wide. Its screen says what's wrong and where (a fork, a gap in the shell, too few coils, no solenoid).

The build guide. Place the Tokamak Control first, facing you. While you hold any ring part (or Pressure Glass) within 48 blocks, it draws the ring it wants behind it, like an altar showing where its pillars go: each missing block outlined in its part's colour (grey vessel, copper coil, dark divertor, orange solenoid), a wrong part with a smaller outline, anything in the way in red. Set the radius on its screen with - and + (default 8). The guide goes once the ring forms.

Fuel. An Isotope Separator processing cube (like the Electrolyser; Titanium, Cryo Coils, Circuit Boards and a Hydrogen Tank) turns 8 Hydrogen Canisters into a Deuterium Canister (120 s and 500 kW per core). The other half is Helium-3 from the Moon's Regolith Sifter. Load one of each into the Control; Item Pipes keep it stocked with four of each.

Ignition and burning. Press Ignite: the solenoid draws a fifth of the ring's rating from the grid for 30 seconds (it waits, and says so, if the grid can't give 95% of that). Then the ring runs itself as a power source: 10 MW for every block of plasma channel, less a tenth for its own coils. The channel is 8 blocks for every block of radius, so a radius-8 ring (64 channel blocks) is rated 640 MW and gives the grid about 576 MW, and a radius-24 ring is rated 1.92 GW (about 1.73 GW to the grid), three times an uprated 10-cube of fission. It burns a pair of canisters every 10 minutes for each 100 MW it delivers (a full radius-8 ring about 35 pairs an hour), so a big ring needs a steady Helium-3 supply from the Moon. Ignition draws a fifth of the rating, so lighting a big ring needs a big grid (128 MW for radius 8). Inside, through a Pressure Glass window, the plasma glows pink-violet, and the ring hums.

Heat and the quench. A burning ring sheds 2% of what it gives the grid as heat (11.5 MW for a full radius-8 ring, about fifty Chillers; about 35 MW at radius 24), split over its Divertors that sit on a coolant loop with sinks. If they can't take it all for 5 seconds, it quenches: a bang, sparks out of the shell, and it goes dark. So does breaking the ring or running out of fuel. Nothing is damaged; you ignite it again (another 30 seconds from the grid). The screen shows the heat, how much the Divertors take and a countdown; the Ops Terminal raises a red alert while it's short and after a quench until it's lit again. Shut down puts it out quietly. None of the fusion parts are on the Exchange.
